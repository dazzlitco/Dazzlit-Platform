## Google Local Services Ads: what you get

Google Local Services Ads (LSA) put your business at the very top of Google when someone nearby searches for the service you offer — above the regular ads and above the organic results. They are built for local service businesses: plumbers, electricians, lawyers, dentists, cleaners, locksmiths, HVAC and many more. Dazzlit sets up and manages your Local Services Ads so they bring in real, bookable leads.

What you get is the whole service. We set up your Local Services profile, guide you through Google's verification and background checks, choose the services and areas you want to cover, manage your budget and bids, and keep the profile healthy — reviews, responsiveness and lead quality all affect how often your ads show.

LSA works differently from ordinary [Google PPC](/resources/guides/google-ppc/). You do not pay for clicks. You pay for leads — phone calls and messages from people who contacted you through the ad. And the ad itself is different too: it shows your business name, your rating, your review count, your hours and a Google Screened or Google Guaranteed badge, so the person searching can judge you before they call. Trust is built into the format, which is why these ads convert well for local services.

That trust is also why the setup matters. A half-finished profile, a missing badge, or unanswered leads will quietly push your ads down the page. Our job is to get the profile right and keep it right.

## Our Google Local Services Ads process

The work starts with discovery. We look at your business, the services you offer, the areas you cover, and how enquiries come in now. We also check whether Local Services Ads are available for your service categories in your area — Google supports a specific list of categories, and it varies by location — and whether your business can meet the verification requirements.

Then we build the profile. Your business information, services, service areas, hours and photos are set up carefully, because the profile is what customers see. We help you through Google's verification: licence checks, insurance checks and background checks, depending on your trade and location. Passing verification earns the Google Screened badge for professional services or the Google Guaranteed badge for home services, and that badge is a real advantage — it tells the searcher that Google has checked you.

Next we set the budget and the targeting. You choose how much you want to spend per week, and Google shows your ads to people searching for your services in your areas. Bidding in LSA is simpler than in standard Google Ads — you set a weekly budget and Google manages the rest — but the settings still matter: which services are switched on, which areas are covered, and how the budget is spread.

Reviews deserve their own attention, because they do more in LSA than anywhere else in paid search. Your rating and review count appear in the ad itself, so a business with many recent five-star reviews will be picked over one with a handful of old ones, even at the same budget. We help you build a routine for asking happy customers for reviews, and we watch for new reviews so nothing goes unanswered. A single unanswered complaint can sit in the ad for weeks, so we treat review management as part of the campaign, not an afterthought.

Then we launch, and the management begins. We review the leads every week: which ones were genuine, which ones were wrong numbers or out-of-area, and which services are producing the best work. Leads that were not genuine can be disputed with Google, and we handle that process for you. We also keep on top of reviews, because your rating is one of the main things that decides how often your ads appear — and we help you build a steady flow of new reviews from happy customers.

Responsiveness matters too. Google tracks how quickly you answer calls and messages, and businesses that respond fast are shown more often. We monitor that and flag it when it slips, so a busy week does not quietly cost you visibility.

## Deliverables & reporting

A typical engagement includes the profile setup, the verification support, the service and area configuration, weekly lead review and disputes, review management and monthly reporting. We also coordinate LSA with your other local marketing — your Google Business Profile, your [Google Maps ads](/resources/guides/google-maps-ads/) and your standard Google Ads campaigns — so the channels reinforce each other instead of competing.

Reporting is monthly and built around the numbers that matter: leads, booked jobs, cost per lead and cost per acquisition, broken down by service and area. We also report on your review rating and count, your response rate, and how many leads were disputed, so you can see the full picture of what the ads are producing.

You own the profile and the data. The Local Services account is yours, the leads are yours, and the monthly report explains what we did, what it achieved and what we plan next.

## Pricing & engagement models

There is no single price for Local Services Ads, because the cost depends on your market. What affects the price is straightforward: which services you advertise, how large your service area is, how much competition there is for leads in your area, and how much management the profile needs. A single trade in one town costs less to run than several services across a whole region.

The media cost works on a pay-per-lead basis, so you pay when someone contacts you through the ad, not when they click. Most clients work with us on a monthly retainer, which covers the weekly lead review, disputes, review management and reporting. A project-based setup is available when you need the profile built and verified and plan to manage it in-house afterwards. The media spend is always separate from the management fee, and you see exactly what it produced.

Your proposal sets out the expected lead cost for your services and area, the management fee and what is included, so you can judge the cost against the jobs it should bring in.
