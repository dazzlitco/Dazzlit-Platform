## PPC Management for Law Firms: what you get

PPC management for law firms is paid search run for a single purpose: more consultations with the right clients. Dazzlit builds and manages Google Ads and Microsoft Ads campaigns for firms of every size, from solo practices to multi-office firms, with every campaign structured around your practice areas and the clients you actually want.

What you get is a complete service, not just ads switched on. We research the keywords your prospective clients use, build campaigns by practice area, write the ad copy, build the landing pages, set up conversion tracking so every enquiry is counted, and then manage the campaigns week by week — adjusting bids, adding negative keywords, testing new ads and cutting what does not work.

Legal marketing has its own rules. Someone searching for a lawyer usually needs help now, and they compare firms fast. So the work is not only about winning the click. The ad has to match the search, the landing page has to answer the question behind it, and the path to calling or booking a consultation has to be obvious. That is what our [law firm marketing](/industries/law-firms/) work is built around: campaigns that turn searches into signed clients, not just traffic.

## Law Firm PPC

Law firm PPC is pay-per-click advertising for legal services, and it works differently from most other kinds of [pay per click advertising](/blog/pay-per-click-advertising/). The person searching is often in a stressful situation — an accident, a divorce, a criminal charge, a business dispute — and they are choosing a firm they will trust with something serious. That means the ad copy, the landing page and the follow-up all matter as much as the bid.

It is also one of the most competitive corners of paid search. Some [expensive ppc keywords](/resources/guides/expensive-ppc-keywords/) in legal — personal injury terms in large US cities, for example — carry very high costs per click, because every firm in the market is bidding for the same small number of high-value cases. The [most expensive ppc keywords](/blog/most-expensive-ppc-keywords/) in law are a reminder that budget alone does not win: Quality Score, ad relevance and landing page experience decide how far each dollar goes.

That is why structure matters. We separate campaigns by practice area — personal injury, family law, criminal defence, employment, corporate and commercial — so the keywords, ads and landing pages stay tightly matched. A person searching for a divorce lawyer should never land on a general "our services" page. We also separate by location, because a firm that serves one city should not pay for clicks from the other side of the country.

Match types and negative keywords do the quiet work here. Exact and phrase match keep spend on searches that show real intent, while a maintained negative keyword list stops the budget leaking into searches for free legal advice, jobs at law firms, or cases you do not take. Bidding strategies are set by goal — maximising conversions where the data supports it, or a target cost per acquisition once enough conversions have been recorded — and adjusted as the evidence builds.

Remarketing has a place too, handled carefully. Someone who read your page on personal injury claims but did not call is worth a second, relevant ad. Done well, it keeps your firm in front of people who are still deciding, without following them around the internet.

## Our PPC for Lawyers process

Every engagement starts with discovery. We look at your current accounts if you have them, your website and intake process, your practice areas and the cases you want more of, and the competitors bidding in your markets. The aim is a clear picture of where enquiries come from now and what a good case is worth to you.

Then comes research. We build the keyword plan by practice area and intent — not just the obvious terms, but the longer, specific searches people type when they have a real problem, such as a practice area plus a city, or a question about their situation. We also map the competitive landscape: who is bidding, what their ads promise, and where there is room to win.

Next we build. Campaigns are structured by practice area and location, with ad groups built around tightly related keywords. Ad copy is written for the person searching, not for the firm — it answers the question, states what happens next, and uses ad extensions (assets) such as call, location and sitelink extensions to give people more ways to reach you. Landing pages are built or improved so the message matches the ad and the consultation booking step is easy to find.

Conversion tracking is set up before anything goes live. Form submissions, calls and consultation bookings are all counted, so we know which keywords and ads produce enquiries and which only produce clicks. Without that, optimisation is guesswork.

Once the campaigns launch, the work becomes weekly management. We review search terms, add negative keywords, test new ad copy, adjust bids by device, time and location, and shift budget toward the campaigns that bring in consultations at an acceptable cost. You hear from us every month with a report and a plain explanation of what changed and why.

## Deliverables & reporting

You always know what you are paying for. A typical engagement includes the account build or rebuild, the keyword plan, ad copy, landing page recommendations, conversion tracking setup, and ongoing weekly management. [PPC trends](/blog/ppc-trends/) change often — new ad formats, new bidding options, new automation — and part of the service is keeping your campaigns current with them rather than leaving them to run unchanged.

Reporting is monthly and built around the numbers that matter to a firm: enquiries, consultations booked, cost per lead and cost per acquisition, broken down by practice area and campaign. We also report on the leading indicators — click-through rate (CTR), cost per click (CPC), Quality Score and impression share — so you can see why performance moved, not just that it did.

You get access to the ad accounts, so nothing is hidden. The data is yours, the campaigns are built in your accounts, and the monthly report explains what we did, what it achieved, and what we plan to do next.

## Pricing & engagement models

There are no fixed prices for law firm PPC, because the cost depends on your market. What affects the price is straightforward: how competitive your practice areas are, how many locations you serve, how many campaigns are needed, and whether landing pages and tracking need to be built or are already in place. A single practice area in one city costs less to run than five practice areas across several cities.

Most firms work with us on a monthly retainer, which covers the ongoing management: weekly optimisation, ad testing, reporting and the regular calls. A project-based setup is available when a firm needs an account built or rebuilt and then managed in-house. Either way, the media budget is separate from the management fee, and it is spent in your own ad accounts — you always see where it went.

Your proposal sets out the media budget, the management fee and what is included, so you can judge the cost against the value of the cases it should bring in.
