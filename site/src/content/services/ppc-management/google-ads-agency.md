## Google Ads Agency: what you get

Dazzlit is a Google Ads agency that manages paid search for businesses that want more leads and sales, not just more clicks. We plan, build and manage Google Ads campaigns — Search, Performance Max, Display, Shopping, video and app campaigns — and we report on what they produce: enquiries, sales and cost per acquisition.

What you get is a full service. We research the keywords your customers use, structure the account around your products, services and locations, write the ad copy, build or improve the landing pages, set up conversion tracking, and manage the campaigns every week. That includes testing new ads, adding negative keywords, adjusting bids and moving budget toward the campaigns that work.

Google Ads changes constantly. New campaign types appear, match types are redefined, automation takes over more of the bidding, and the features that worked last year quietly stop working. Part of what you pay for is a team that keeps up with those changes and applies them to your account, rather than a campaign that was set up once and left to run.

Whether you are starting from scratch or taking over an account that has been running for years, the approach is the same: understand what a customer is worth to you, build the account around intent, track every conversion, and keep improving on the evidence.

## Google Ads Specialist

A Google Ads specialist is someone who manages paid search every day, and that focus shows in the results. Google Ads has hundreds of settings — bidding strategies, audience signals, ad assets, match types, location and device adjustments — and the difference between a good account and a mediocre one is usually in the details: the search terms that get excluded, the ads that get tested, the landing pages that get fixed.

Your campaigns are run by a specialist, not a generalist. They know how Quality Score affects what you pay per click, when to use Performance Max and when to keep control with standard Search campaigns, and how to read the data to tell the difference between a campaign that needs more budget and one that needs a better offer.

That matters because Google Ads rewards relevance. An ad that matches the search, a landing page that answers the question, and a clear next step all raise Quality Score, which lowers your cost per click and improves your position. A specialist's job is to keep all three aligned, week after week.

If you have been managing ads yourself, a specialist also brings a second pair of eyes. Most self-managed accounts we audit have the same problems: broad keywords burning budget, no negative keyword list, conversion tracking that misses phone calls, and landing pages that do not match the ads. Fixing those four things alone often improves performance more than any increase in budget.

## Google PPC Agency

As a [Google PPC](/resources/guides/google-ppc/) agency, we treat Google Ads as part of a wider paid media plan, not as a standalone channel. Search captures people who are ready to buy. Performance Max and Display reach people who are still deciding. Shopping puts your products in front of people comparing prices. Video builds awareness that search later converts. The mix depends on your business, and it changes as the data comes in.

We also run Microsoft Ads alongside Google for many clients. The audience is smaller, but the cost per click is often lower and the users are different — particularly in business-to-business markets. It is not right for everyone, but where it fits, it is a useful second source of leads from the same keyword research.

[How does Google Ads work](/blog/how-does-google-ads-work/)? In simple terms, you bid to show ads when someone searches for something related to your business, and you pay when they click. But the auction is not won by the highest bid alone. Google weighs the bid, the quality of the ad and the experience of the landing page. That is why two advertisers can pay very different amounts for the same click — and why the work of an agency is as much about relevance as it is about budget.

If you are weighing up whether paid search is right for you, [are Google Ads worth it](/blog/are-google-ads-worth-it/) is a fair question, and the honest answer is that it depends on your margins, your market and how well the account is run. That is exactly what a free proposal is for: we look at your keywords, your competitors and your numbers, and tell you plainly whether the maths works.

## Our Google Ads Agency process

The work starts with discovery. We review your existing account if you have one, look at your website and how enquiries come in, and agree on what a lead or a sale is worth to you. That number shapes every decision that follows, from which keywords to bid on to how much to spend.

Then we research. We build the keyword plan around intent: the searches people make when they are ready to buy, the searches they make when they are comparing, and the searches that only waste budget. We look at who else is bidding, what their ads say, and what it will take to win. We also check what your landing pages promise, because an ad can only do its job if the page it leads to does its job too.

Then we build. Campaigns are structured by product, service and location. Ad groups stay tight, so the ad always matches the keyword. Ad copy is written around the customer's question, with ad extensions (assets) — sitelinks, callouts, call and location extensions — to give people more reasons and more ways to act. Conversion tracking is set up before launch, covering form submissions, calls, purchases and any other action that matters to you.

After launch, management is weekly. We check the search terms report and add negative keywords, test new ad copy against the old, adjust bids by device, location and time of day, and move budget toward the campaigns producing results at the right cost. Remarketing audiences are built from your site visitors, so people who looked but did not convert see a relevant follow-up ad.

You hear from us every month with a report and a call, and you can reach the person running your account whenever you need to.

You own the accounts and the data. ## Pricing & engagement models

There is no single price for Google Ads management, because the work depends on the size and complexity of your account. What affects the price is straightforward: how many campaigns are needed, how many locations and products you cover, whether landing pages and tracking need building, and how competitive your keywords are.
