## Copywriting Services: what you get

Copywriting is the writing that makes your marketing work. It is the words on your website, in your ads, in your emails, and across every piece of content you publish. Good copy does two jobs at once: it explains what you offer in language your buyers understand, and it gives them a clear reason to act.

Our [copywriting](/resources/guides/copywriting/) service covers the writing your business needs most. That usually means website copy and landing pages, product and service pages, ad copy, email sequences, and sales pages. It can also include case studies, brochures, and the scripts for short videos. Every piece is written for a specific reader at a specific stage of the buyer journey, so the words do more than fill a page — they move someone closer to contacting you.

We start with a content audit of what you already have. Some pages are worth keeping and improving; some are working against you and need a rewrite; some are missing entirely. The audit tells us where the gaps are, and the plan fills them in the order that matters most: the pages closest to a sale first, then the pages that bring people in.

## SEO Copywriting Services

[SEO copywriting](/resources/guides/seo-copywriting/) is copywriting written to rank as well as to convert. A page that ranks but does not persuade wastes the traffic it earns. A page that persuades but never gets found wastes the writing. The work is to do both.

We start from search intent: what the person typing the query actually wants. Some searches want a definition, some want a comparison, and some want a supplier. The copy answers the question behind the query in full, in the order the reader needs it, and then makes the next step obvious. Around that, we build topic clusters — groups of related pages that cover a subject properly — with pillar pages that tie them together. That structure helps search engines understand what your site is about, and it helps readers find the next thing they need.

We also write for E-E-A-T: experience, expertise, authoritativeness, and trust. That means real detail instead of general claims, named authors where it helps, and facts a reader can check. Thin, generic copy does not rank and does not convert. Specific copy does both.

## SEO Copywriting Agency

Hiring a copywriting agency is different from hiring a freelance writer. A writer produces pages; an agency produces a programme. That means strategy first, then an editorial calendar, then the writing, then distribution and measurement. The calendar sets out what gets written, in what order, and why — so the work builds on itself instead of arriving as a set of disconnected pages.

It also means the copy connects with your other marketing. The same messages run through your search pages, your social posts, and your paid ads, so a buyer hears one story wherever they meet you. And because the work is managed as a programme, you get reporting that shows what the copy is doing: which pages bring in traffic, which ones bring in enquiries, and what to write next.

If you are weighing the two kinds of writing, our guide to [copywriting vs content writing](/blog/copywriting-vs-content-writing/) explains the difference and when each one matters. Copywriting is the writing that asks the reader to act. Content writing is the writing that earns their attention first. Most businesses need both, and the balance depends on where your buyers are.

## Website Copywriting Services

Your website is where most of your marketing ends up, so the copy on it carries the most weight. Website copywriting covers your home page, your service and product pages, your about page, and the landing pages your ads point to. Each page has one job, and the copy is built around it.

A home page has to explain what you do, who it is for, and why you are the right choice — in seconds. A service page has to answer the questions a buyer asks before they enquire, and then ask for the enquiry. A landing page has to do one thing only, with nothing on it that distracts from it. We write each page for its job, and we write it in your buyers' language, not your industry's jargon.

Web copywriting also covers the small words people overlook: the headings, the buttons, the form labels, and the error messages. These are the words that decide whether someone finishes the journey or gives up halfway through it. Getting them right is a large part of what turns visits into leads.

## Our Copywriting Services process

Every engagement follows the same five steps.

First, discovery. We learn how your business works, who your buyers are, what they worry about, and what your competitors are saying. We look at your analytics to see which pages already work and which ones lose people.

Second, strategy. We agree the messages that matter, the pages to write or rewrite, and the order to do them in. This is where the content strategy takes shape: the topics, the formats, and how each piece fits the buyer journey.

Third, writing. Each piece is written from a brief that sets out the reader, the search intent, the key points, and the action it should lead to. Drafts go through an edit for accuracy, clarity, and voice before you see them.

Fourth, publishing and distribution. Copy is placed on the page with the right headings, links, and calls to action, and then shared through the channels where your buyers will see it — search, social, and email.

Fifth, measurement. We track how each page performs and use that to decide what to write next. Copywriting is not a one-off task; it is a programme that improves as the evidence builds.

## Deliverables & reporting

What you receive each month depends on the scope we agree, but it always includes the finished copy, ready to publish, and a report that shows what it is doing.

The copy itself arrives with the headings, meta descriptions, and internal links it needs, so it can go live without extra work from your team. Longer pieces come with a summary of the sources and claims used, so your team can check anything that needs checking.

The report covers the numbers that matter: traffic to the pages we wrote, the enquiries and sales they produced, and the cost per acquisition. We also track content ROI — what the programme costs against the revenue it brings in — because copywriting has to pay for itself like any other channel. Rankings and traffic are reported too, as supporting detail, but the headline is always leads and revenue.

## Pricing & engagement models

The cost of copywriting depends on what is involved. The main factors are how much you need written, how much research each piece requires, and how many channels the copy has to serve. A set of landing pages needs less research than a series of long-form guides, and a single-channel programme costs less than one that feeds search, social, and email at the same time.

We work in two ways. A monthly retainer suits businesses that publish regularly and want the programme to keep building — strategy, an editorial calendar, and a steady flow of new and updated copy. A project engagement suits a defined job with a clear end, such as a website rewrite or a set of landing pages for a launch. Either way, your proposal sets out the scope, the deliverables, and the fee before any work starts, so there are no surprises.
