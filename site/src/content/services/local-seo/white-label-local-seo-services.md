## White Label Local SEO Services: what you get

White label local SEO means your clients get specialist local search work, and your agency gets the credit. Dazzlit does the work behind the scenes — Google Business Profile optimisation, local citations, review strategy, location pages, and local schema — while your agency keeps the client relationship, the reporting, and its name on everything the client sees.

It is built for agencies that sell local SEO but do not have a local search team in-house, or whose team is already full. Instead of turning work away, hiring in a hurry, or handing a client a thin service, you plug in a team that does local SEO all day, every day, under your name.

What your clients get is the full [local SEO](/resources/guides/local-seo/) service: a Google Business Profile set up and managed properly, a consistent name, address, and phone number (NAP) across the directories that matter, citations built and cleaned up, a plan for earning local reviews and responding to them, service-area and location pages that give each place they serve something to rank for, and the technical work — including local schema — that helps search engines understand where they operate and what they do.

What you get is a service you can sell with confidence: clear deliverables, regular reporting you can pass on to your clients, and a single point of contact on our side. Your clients never need to know another agency is involved, and the work is delivered to your standards, in your voice, on your schedule.

This works for digital agencies, web design firms, consultants, and marketing companies that want to offer local SEO without building a department for it. Whether you have one client who needs help getting found locally or a whole book of local businesses, the work scales to fit.

It also protects your margins. Because the work is done by specialists who already have the process, the tools, and the checklists in place, you are not paying for someone else's learning curve — and you can price the service to your client with confidence, knowing the delivery cost is fixed and the quality is consistent.

## Our White Label Local SEO Services process

Every engagement starts with an audit. We look at the client's Google Business Profile, their rankings in the map pack and organic results, their NAP consistency across the web, their reviews, their website, and the competitors ranking ahead of them. The audit shows what is holding them back and what will move the needle first.

From there we agree a plan with you: which locations and service areas to prioritise, what the first three months of work looks like, and what success means for that client. The plan is built around how Google actually ranks local businesses — proximity, relevance, and prominence — so the work focuses on the things that change rankings, not the things that only look busy.

Then the foundations go in. The Google Business Profile is completed and optimised, with the right categories, services, hours, and photos. NAP details are corrected wherever they are wrong or inconsistent. Citations are built on the directories that matter for the client's industry and area, and duplicates are cleaned up. Bing Places and Apple Business Connect are covered too, so the business is findable beyond Google.

Next comes content and reviews. Location and service-area pages are written or improved so each place the client serves has something real to rank for. A review process is set up so the client steadily earns new reviews and responds to them properly. Local schema is added so search engines can read the business details directly from the site.

After that it is a monthly rhythm: keep the profile active, keep building citations and content, keep earning reviews, and keep checking what is working. Our [local SEO checklist](/resources/checklists/local-seo-checklist/) is the backbone of that routine — the same checks, every month, for every client.

## Pricing & engagement models

White label pricing depends on scope. The main factors are how many locations each client has, how many service areas need coverage, how competitive their market is, and how much foundation work is needed at the start — a business with a neglected profile and inconsistent listings needs more upfront work than one that is already in decent shape.

There are two common ways to work with us. The first is a monthly retainer per client, which covers the ongoing work: profile management, citations, reviews, content, and reporting. The second is project-based work, such as a one-off audit and setup, or a fixed block of work to fix a specific problem. Many partners start with a project for one client and move to retainers once they see how it runs.

Our [local SEO packages](/services/local-seo/local-seo-pricing-and-packages/) page explains what affects price in more detail. For an exact figure for a specific client, send us the details and we will put together a free proposal — there is no charge for the proposal itself, and no obligation.
