## SEO for Home Services & Contractors: what you get

When a pipe bursts or a boiler breaks, nobody browses. They search on their phone: "emergency plumber near me", "roof repair Chicago", "electrician open now". SEO for home services and contractors is the work of making sure your business is the one they find — in the map pack, in the organic results, and on a Google Business Profile that answers their questions before they even call.

What you get from Dazzlit is local SEO built around how people actually choose a trades business. That means a fully optimised Google Business Profile, consistent name, address, and phone number (NAP) details across the directories that matter, local citations, a review strategy that steadily earns new customer reviews, service-area and location pages that give each service and each place you cover a real page to rank for, and the technical work — including local schema — that helps search engines understand where you work and what you do.

It also means content written for the person searching, not just for the search engine. Someone with a burst pipe has different worries from someone planning a kitchen remodel or comparing builders for an extension. Our pages are written for those people first, and for rankings second — with clear service descriptions, honest explanations, and an easy way to get in touch.

## SEO for Plumbers

Plumbing searches are urgent, local, and usually made from a phone. "Emergency plumber near me" is the classic example, and the businesses that win it are the ones in the map pack with strong reviews, a complete profile, and a phone number that is easy to find and tap.

SEO for plumbers therefore starts with the map pack and the profile. We make sure the business appears for the services and areas it actually covers, that the profile shows the right services, hours, and service area, and that reviews are being earned steadily — because for a trade where a stranger is coming into someone's home, reviews are the trust signal that decides the call.

Then come the service pages: emergency plumbing, drain cleaning, boiler repair, bathroom fitting, and whatever else the business offers, each with its own page, written properly, covering the questions customers ask before they book. Location and service-area pages do the same for each town or neighbourhood covered, so the business ranks across its whole patch rather than just its home town.

## SEO for Construction Companies

Construction searches are slower and higher value. A homeowner planning an extension or a developer looking for a contractor will research for weeks, compare several firms, and check past work before making contact. That makes construction SEO a mix of local visibility and proof: ranking for "builders near me" and "construction company Chicago", and backing it up with pages that show what the firm builds, where it works, and how to start a project.

The work starts with the same local foundations — Google Business Profile, NAP consistency, citations, reviews — but the content goes deeper. Service pages for extensions, renovations, new builds, and commercial work, each written properly. Location pages for each area served. And project content that answers the questions people ask before they commit: how long a typical project takes, what the process looks like, and what happens at the first site visit.

For construction, reviews and photos carry unusual weight. A firm with a strong review record and a profile full of real project photos looks like a safe choice next to a competitor with an empty profile. We build the review process and keep the profile active, so the proof keeps growing.

## Our SEO for Contractors process

We start with an audit: your rankings in the map pack and organic results, your Google Business Profile, your reviews, your NAP consistency, your website, and the firms ranking ahead of you for each service. The audit shows what is holding you back and what will move the needle first.

Then we agree a plan: which services and areas to prioritise, what the first three months look like, and what success means for your business — usually measured in job enquiries and booked work, not just rankings.

The foundations come next. Your Google Business Profile is completed and optimised, with the right categories, services, and hours. NAP details are corrected everywhere they are wrong. Citations are built on the trade and local directories that matter, and duplicates are cleaned up.

Then content and reviews. Service and service-area pages are written or rebuilt. A review process is set up so the business steadily earns new reviews and responds to them properly — including a plan for the difficult ones, which every business gets eventually. Local schema is added so search engines can read the business details directly.

After that it is a monthly rhythm: keep the profile active, keep building citations and content, keep earning reviews, and keep checking what is working. Every month the plan is adjusted based on what the data shows.
