## SEO for Law Firms: what you get

When someone needs a lawyer, they search. Usually on their phone, usually with a place attached: "divorce lawyer near me", "personal injury attorney Chicago", "criminal defense lawyer open now". SEO for law firms is the work of making sure your firm is the one they find — in the map pack, in the organic results, and on a Google Business Profile that answers their questions before they even call.

What you get from Dazzlit is local SEO built around how people actually choose a lawyer. That means a fully optimised Google Business Profile, consistent name, address, and phone number (NAP) details across the directories that matter, local citations, a review strategy that steadily earns new client reviews, location and practice-area pages that give each service a real page to rank for, and the technical work — including local schema — that helps search engines understand where you practise and what you do.

It also means content that speaks to the person searching, not just to the search engine. Someone looking for a family lawyer has different worries from someone looking for a personal injury attorney. Our pages are written for those people first, and for rankings second.

## Law Firm SEO

Law firm SEO is search optimisation for legal services. In practice that means ranking for the searches that bring in clients: practice-area terms in your city ("estate planning attorney Austin"), problem-based terms ("what to do after a car accident"), and the firm's own name. The first two are where new clients come from; the third is where you protect the reputation you already have.

The work covers the whole site, not just the homepage. Each practice area gets its own page, written properly, with the questions clients actually ask. Each office location gets its own page. The site is made fast, easy to use on a phone, and easy for a stressed person to contact you from — because most legal searches happen in a difficult moment, and the firm that is easiest to reach usually gets the call.

Good law firm SEO also respects the rules of the profession. Legal advertising is regulated, and what you can claim varies by jurisdiction. We write carefully, avoid promises no lawyer should make, and keep your content accurate — so the marketing never creates a problem for the practice.

## Legal SEO

Legal SEO is the same discipline, looked at from the content side. Google treats legal topics as "your money or your life" subjects, which means it holds legal content to a higher standard than most. Thin pages, copied text, or generic advice will not rank — and should not.

So the content we build for law firms is specific: what the law says in plain language, what the process looks like, what it costs to find out more, and what happens at the first consultation. It is written to be genuinely useful to someone facing a legal problem, and it is structured so search engines can see exactly which questions each page answers.

That depth is also what earns links and citations from other sites — local news, legal directories, community organisations — which in turn strengthens the firm's authority in search. Legal SEO is slow work done properly, and it is the work that still ranks years later.

## Local SEO for Lawyers

Most law firm clients come from nearby. That is why local SEO matters more for lawyers than for almost any other profession: the map pack — the three local results with the map at the top of a search — is where a large share of enquiries start.

Google ranks those three on proximity, relevance, and prominence. Proximity is how close the firm is to the searcher. Relevance is how well the firm's profile and website match what was searched for. Prominence is how well-known and well-reviewed the firm is. You cannot change where your office is, but the other two are work: the right categories and services on your profile, complete and consistent listings, genuine reviews, local citations, and content that proves you do the work people are searching for.

We cover the full local picture: Google Business Profile management, Bing Places, and Apple Business Connect, so the firm is findable wherever people look. And because many firms serve several towns or neighbourhoods, we build service-area and location pages that let each place the firm covers rank on its own merits.

## Our SEO for Lawyers process

We start with an audit: your rankings in the map pack and organic results, your Google Business Profile, your reviews, your NAP consistency, your website, and the firms ranking ahead of you in each practice area. The audit shows what is holding you back and what will move the needle first.

Then we agree a plan: which practice areas and locations to prioritise, what the first three months look like, and what success means for your firm — usually measured in consultation enquiries, not just rankings.

The foundations come next. Your Google Business Profile is completed and optimised, with the right categories, services, and hours. NAP details are corrected everywhere they are wrong. Citations are built on the legal and local directories that matter, and duplicates are cleaned up.

Then content and reviews. Practice-area and location pages are written or rebuilt. A review process is set up so the firm steadily earns new reviews and responds to them properly — including a plan for the difficult ones, which every firm gets eventually. Local schema is added so search engines can read the firm's details directly.

After that it is a monthly rhythm: keep the profile active, keep building citations and content, keep earning reviews, and keep checking what is working. Every month the plan is adjusted based on what the data shows.
