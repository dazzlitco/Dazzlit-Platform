## SEO for Dentists: what you get

When someone needs a dentist, they search. Usually on their phone, usually with a place attached: "dentist near me", "emergency dentist open now", "Invisalign dentist Chicago". SEO for dentists is the work of making sure your practice is the one they find — in the map pack, in the organic results, and on a Google Business Profile that answers their questions before they even call.

What you get from Dazzlit is local SEO built around how people actually choose a dentist. That means a fully optimised Google Business Profile, consistent name, address, and phone number (NAP) details across the directories that matter, local citations, a review strategy that steadily earns new patient reviews, service and location pages that give each treatment a real page to rank for, and the technical work — including local schema — that helps search engines understand where you practise and what you offer.

It also means content written for the person searching, not just for the search engine. Someone looking for a family dentist has different worries from someone in pain looking for an emergency appointment, or a parent looking for a children's dentist. Our pages are written for those people first, and for rankings second.

## Local SEO for Dentists

Most new patients come from nearby. That is why local SEO matters more for dentists than for almost any other profession: the map pack — the three local results with the map at the top of a search — is where a large share of new-patient enquiries start.

Google ranks those three on proximity, relevance, and prominence. Proximity is how close the practice is to the searcher. Relevance is how well the practice's profile and website match what was searched for. Prominence is how well-known and well-reviewed the practice is. You cannot change where your practice is, but the other two are work: the right categories and services on your profile, complete and consistent listings, genuine reviews, local citations, and content that proves you offer the treatments people are searching for.

We cover the full local picture: Google Business Profile management, Bing Places, and Apple Business Connect, so the practice is findable wherever people look. And because many practices serve several neighbourhoods or towns, we build service-area and location pages that let each place the practice covers rank on its own merits.

## Dental Practice SEO

Dental practice SEO is search optimisation for a dental business. In practice that means ranking for the searches that bring in patients: treatment terms in your area ("dental implants Austin", "teeth whitening near me"), problem-based terms ("toothache what to do"), and the practice's own name. The first two are where new patients come from; the third is where you protect the reputation you already have.

The work covers the whole site, not just the homepage. Each service — general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, implants, emergency care — gets its own page, written properly, with the questions patients actually ask. Each location gets its own page. The site is made fast, easy to use on a phone, and easy for a nervous person to book from — because many dental searches happen when someone is in pain or anxious, and the practice that is easiest to reach usually gets the call.

Good dental SEO also respects the patient. Health information must be accurate, claims must be careful, and the content should help people make a decision — not pressure them. We write accordingly.

## Our SEO for Dentists process

We start with an audit: your rankings in the map pack and organic results, your Google Business Profile, your reviews, your NAP consistency, your website, and the practices ranking ahead of you for each treatment. The audit shows what is holding you back and what will move the needle first.

Then we agree a plan: which treatments and locations to prioritise, what the first three months look like, and what success means for your practice — usually measured in new-patient enquiries, not just rankings.

The foundations come next. Your Google Business Profile is completed and optimised, with the right categories, services, and hours. NAP details are corrected everywhere they are wrong. Citations are built on the dental and local directories that matter, and duplicates are cleaned up.

Then content and reviews. Service and location pages are written or rebuilt. A review process is set up so the practice steadily earns new reviews and responds to them properly — including a plan for the difficult ones, which every practice gets eventually. Local schema is added so search engines can read the practice's details directly.

After that it is a monthly rhythm: keep the profile active, keep building citations and content, keep earning reviews, and keep checking what is working. Every month the plan is adjusted based on what the data shows.
