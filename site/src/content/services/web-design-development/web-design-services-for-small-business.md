## Web Design Services for Small Business: what you get

For a small business, the website is often the first thing a customer sees and the last thing they check before getting in touch. It has to explain what you do, show that you can be trusted, and make it easy to call, book, or buy. If it fails at any of those, the customer moves on to the next business in the search results.

Our web design services for small business build websites that do that work. You get a site designed around your customers and your goals, built on a content management system you can update yourself, and set up so people can find it on Google. Every project includes strategy, UX and UI design, copywriting support, development, technical SEO foundations, and launch. The design is responsive and mobile-first, because most of your visitors will arrive on a phone, and it meets accessibility (WCAG) standards so everyone can use it.

We keep the process practical for a small team. You approve the design before we build it, you get training so you can update pages yourself, and you get monthly reporting that shows what the site is producing in enquiries, calls, and sales. If you are starting from scratch, our [how to design a website](/blog/how-to-design-a-website/) guide explains the decisions involved; if you already have a site, we can rebuild it or improve what is there.

Local visibility is part of the job, not an extra. Most small business customers search with a place in mind, such as a plumber near them or a cafe in their town, so we set the site up for local search: clear service and location pages, consistent business details, and review links that make it easy for happy customers to leave feedback. The site is built to work with your Google Business Profile, so the two reinforce each other instead of competing. We also plan for the ways customers actually reach you, whether that is a phone call, a quote form, a booking link, or a visit to your shop.

## Our Web Design Services for Small Business process

We follow a clear process, so you always know what is happening and when the site will launch.

**Discover.** We start with your business, your customers, and your goals. What should the site do: take bookings, generate quote requests, sell products, or simply get the phone ringing? We audit your current site if you have one, look at what competitors are doing, and map the pages the new site needs. This is also where we agree how success will be measured, usually enquiries, calls, and sales.

**Design.** We plan the information architecture first, so visitors can find what they need in one or two clicks. Then we design wireframes that show the layout of each key page, followed by visual prototypes that show the real design. You review and approve each stage, so there are no surprises at launch. The design covers the homepage, service pages, and contact points, with a consistent look that matches your brand.

**Build.** We develop the approved design into a working website, add your content, and set up forms, analytics, and technical SEO. The site is built in a staging environment you can review, and we test it across browsers and devices. We check Core Web Vitals, the loading and stability measures Google uses, and we make sure the site is fast on mobile connections.

**Launch and improve.** We run through a launch checklist covering redirects, sitemaps, and testing, then move the site live. In the first weeks we watch performance closely, fix anything that needs fixing, and start the monthly reporting. After launch, we keep improving the pages that produce the most enquiries, because a website should get better over time, not stand still.

## Deliverables & reporting

When the project ends, you own the website, the design files, and the content. Your team gets a CMS they can use, a short training session, and written guidance on updating pages and publishing posts.

What you receive depends on scope, but a typical small business project includes the designed and built website, service and location pages, contact and quote forms, analytics and search console setup, and a launch checklist that covers redirects, sitemaps, and testing. We also set up the local search basics, so the site works with your Google Business Profile and your business details are consistent everywhere they appear. We hand over the logins and records for hosting and the domain, so nothing is locked away from you.

Reporting starts at launch. Each month we send a report covering the numbers that matter to a small business: calls, form submissions, quote requests, and sales, plus traffic, search visibility, and which pages produce enquiries. The report also lists what we are improving next, so the site keeps getting better instead of standing still. ## Pricing & engagement models

Small business website costs vary with scope. The main factors are the number of pages, how much of the design is custom, whether you need new copy or photography, and what the site connects to, such as a booking system, a payment provider, or a CRM. A focused brochure site costs less than a larger site with ecommerce, custom tools, and integrations.

We work in three ways. A **project** covers the design and build of the site for a fixed, agreed fee. A **retainer** covers ongoing work after launch, such as new pages, content, and conversion improvements. A **phased** engagement splits a larger build into stages, so you can launch the core site first and add features over time. Your proposal sets out the scope, the fee, and the timeline in writing before work starts, so there are no surprises.
