## WordPress Development: what you get

WordPress powers a large share of the web for a simple reason: it is flexible, your team can update it, and it can grow from a small brochure site into a large content or ecommerce site. The difference between a WordPress site that works and one that does not is how it is built.

Our WordPress development service builds custom WordPress websites that are fast, secure, and easy to manage. You get a site designed around your goals, built on a custom theme rather than a bought template, with the plugins and integrations your business needs and nothing it does not. Every project includes strategy, UX and UI design, development, content, technical SEO, and launch. The design is responsive and mobile-first, meets accessibility (WCAG) standards, and is tested against Core Web Vitals, the loading and stability measures Google uses.

Your team gets an editor they can actually use, with page layouts they can change and posts they can publish without touching code. And because the build is clean and documented, future changes are straightforward rather than fragile. For a closer look at the design side, see our [wordpress website design](/resources/guides/wordpress-website-design/) guide.

## WordPress Website Developer

A WordPress website developer does more than install a theme. The job is to build a site that is fast, secure, and maintainable, and to make sure the parts your team touches every day are simple to use.

We build custom themes, so the design is yours and the code is lean. A bought theme often carries features you will never use, and that extra code slows the site down. A custom theme carries only what your site needs. We choose plugins carefully, preferring well-maintained ones with a clear purpose, and we write custom functionality where a plugin would be the wrong tool. Fewer, better plugins means fewer security risks and fewer conflicts.

Performance is part of the build, not an afterthought. We optimise images, caching, and code, and we test the site on real devices and connections. Security is handled the same way: hardening, updates, backups, and user roles that give each team member only the access they need.

We also plan for the day-to-day. Your team gets training and written guidance, so publishing a post or updating a page is a normal task, not a support ticket. And if you already have a WordPress site that has grown slow or messy, we can rebuild or clean it up, migrating your content safely and keeping your search rankings intact with proper redirects.

## Our WordPress Development process

We follow a clear process, so you always know what is happening and when the site will launch.

**Discover.** We start with your goals, your audience, and the job the site has to do. We audit your current site if you have one, look at competitors, and map the pages and features the new site needs. Technical requirements, such as integrations, hosting, and migration, are agreed here, along with how success will be measured.

**Design.** We plan the information architecture first, then design wireframes that show the layout of each key page. From there we build visual prototypes, so you can see the real design before anything is coded. You review and approve each stage.

**Build.** We develop the approved design into a custom WordPress theme in a staging environment you can review. Content is added, plugins and integrations are configured, and technical SEO, analytics, and accessibility are set up. We test across browsers and devices, and check site speed and Core Web Vitals.

**Launch and improve.** We run through a launch checklist covering redirects, sitemaps, and testing, then move the site live. In the first weeks we watch performance closely, fix anything that needs fixing, and start monthly reporting. After launch, we keep improving the pages that matter most.

## Pricing & engagement models

WordPress development costs vary with scope. The main factors are the number of pages, how much of the design and theme is custom, what the site connects to, and whether you need new copy, photography, or a migration from an existing site. A focused site on a custom theme costs less than a large build with custom features, WooCommerce, and integrations.

We work in three ways. A **project** covers the design and build of the site for a fixed, agreed fee. A **retainer** covers ongoing work after launch, such as new pages, content, updates, and performance improvements. A **phased** engagement splits a larger build into stages, so you can launch the core site first and add features over time. Your proposal sets out the scope, the fee, and the timeline in writing before work starts.
