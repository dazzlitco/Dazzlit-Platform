## B2B Digital Marketing Agency: what you get

B2B buyers do not behave like consumers. They research for weeks, compare several suppliers, and decide as a group. A B2B digital marketing agency builds a programme for that reality: content and campaigns that reach the whole buying committee, across the channels where they actually spend their time.

What you get is a plan built around your sales cycle, not around a single channel. We map the customer journey from the first search to the signed contract, and build the marketing funnel to match it: awareness content that earns attention, mid-funnel content that answers the questions buyers ask before they talk to sales, and conversion pages that make it easy to enquire. Every stage has its own buyer personas, because the person who finds you on Google is rarely the person who signs the contract.

The work covers the full marketing mix. Search and AI search put you in front of buyers who are already looking. Paid media reaches the accounts on your target list. Content and social build the case over time. Email keeps the conversation going between first contact and decision. And your website turns that attention into enquiries your sales team can work with.

A B2B agency is also measured differently. Success is not traffic: it is qualified leads, pipeline value and revenue. We agree KPIs for each stage, from cost per acquisition (CAC) and return on ad spend (ROAS) to lifetime value (LTV) and return on investment (ROI), and we report on them every month.

## B2B Marketing Firm

A B2B marketing firm is a specialist, not a generalist. The difference shows in the work: account lists instead of broad audiences, buying-committee messaging instead of consumer headlines, and content that speaks to the person who approves the budget as well as the person who uses the product.

When you compare firms, look for B2B experience in your market, not just a list of services. Ask how they handle long sales cycles, how they work with your sales team, and how they measure a lead. A firm that reports on form fills alone will send you enquiries your sales team cannot use. A firm that reports on qualified leads, pipeline and revenue will send you business.

Dazzlit works with B2B companies across technology, professional services, manufacturing and healthcare, in six markets. The teams are built around each client: the right industry experience, the right channel specialists and working hours that overlap with yours.

## B2B Advertising Agency

B2B advertising is about reaching the right accounts, not the most people. A B2B ad programme starts with a target account list and builds campaigns around it: search ads for the terms your buyers use, paid social aimed at job titles and companies rather than broad interests, and display that keeps your brand in front of the committee between meetings.

The creative has to work harder in B2B. Your buyer has seen the same claims from every supplier, so the ad has to say something specific: a result, a comparison, a number, a named problem. We test ad angles against each other and keep the ones that produce qualified enquiries, not just clicks.

Attribution matters more here too. A B2B buyer may see five ads, read three guides and attend a webinar before enquiring, and the last click tells you almost nothing about that journey. We set up attribution in GA4 so you can see how the channels work together, and we use first-party data from your CRM to connect campaigns to pipeline and revenue.

## B2B Marketing Consulting

Some B2B companies do not need an agency to run everything. They need a clear plan, an honest audit of what they have, and someone to check the work as it goes. That is B2B marketing consulting: strategy and oversight, with your team or your existing suppliers doing the delivery.

A consulting engagement starts with an audit of your channels, your website, your content and your competitors. From there we build the strategy: the channels, the budget split, the timeline and the targets, all tied to your sales cycle. The plan sets out the buyer personas for each stage of the funnel, the brand positioning that separates you from the alternatives, and the KPIs the plan will be judged on.

Consulting suits B2B teams that want a second opinion before a bigger investment, companies entering a new market, and businesses whose marketing has grown channel by channel without a shared plan. And because Dazzlit also runs campaigns, the advice is grounded in what is working now, and we can take over delivery whenever you want us to.

## Our B2B Digital Marketing Agency process

Every engagement follows the same four steps, sized to your brief.

Discover. We audit what you have: your website, your analytics, your ad accounts, your content and your competitors. We interview your sales team, because they know the objections buyers raise and the questions they ask before they buy. We review your CRM data and check how your brand is positioned against the alternatives your buyers consider.

Plan. We build the strategy: the channels, the budget split, the timeline and the targets. The plan sets out the marketing mix for each stage of the funnel, the audiences and buyer personas for each channel, and the KPIs that will tell us whether it is working. Where account-based marketing fits, we build the account list and the campaigns around it, with an [account based marketing agency](/services/digital-marketing/account-based-marketing-agency/) approach that targets named companies rather than broad audiences.

Build. We set up what the plan needs: tracking and attribution in GA4, landing pages, campaign structures, content calendars and reporting dashboards. If your team will run the plan, we hand over the setup with the documentation and training to use it.

Grow. We review performance every month against the agreed KPIs, and adjust the plan as the data comes in. Channels that produce qualified pipeline get more budget; channels that do not are fixed or paused. The plan changes as your market changes, and as your sales team tells us what the pipeline actually looks like.

## Deliverables & reporting

You always know what you are getting. A B2B engagement delivers a written strategy, the audit findings behind it, a channel-by-channel plan with budgets and timelines, and a reporting dashboard built around your KPIs. Campaigns, content and landing pages are delivered to the agreed schedule, and every deliverable is reviewed against the brief before it goes live.

Reporting is monthly, in plain language. Each report shows qualified leads, pipeline value, sales, revenue and cost per acquisition, with the contribution of each channel. We include what changed, what we did about it and what happens next. Your consultant walks you through the report, so the numbers are always explained by a person who knows your account.

We report on the customer journey, not just the last click, and we connect the reporting to your CRM wherever possible, so a lead can be followed from first touch to closed deal. That is how you know which channels are bringing in business, and which are only bringing in traffic.

## Pricing & engagement models

The cost of a B2B programme depends on the channels in the plan, the markets it covers and whether account-based marketing is part of the scope. A focused search and content programme costs less than a full omnichannel programme across several countries. We scope every engagement to your brief before work starts, so you know the price and the deliverables in advance.

We work in three ways. A monthly retainer covers the ongoing running of your channels, with a dedicated team and monthly reporting. A project engagement covers a defined piece of work, such as a website rebuild, a market-entry plan or a campaign launch, with a fixed scope and a fixed end date. And a consulting engagement gives you the strategy and oversight, with your team doing the delivery.

Most B2B clients start with a retainer on one or two core channels, then add more as the pipeline grows. That keeps the cost tied to the work, and the work tied to the results.
