## Small Business Marketing Services: what you get

Small business marketing is the work of getting found, getting chosen and getting repeat business, on a budget that has to pay for itself. It covers the channels your customers actually use: search, paid ads, social media, content, email and your website. The aim is simple: more enquiries from the right people, at a cost per lead you can afford.

What you get from Dazzlit is a plan built for a small business, not a scaled-down enterprise plan. We start with your goals and your market, then choose the channels that will bring in enquiries fastest for the budget you have. For most small businesses that means one or two core channels first, done properly, rather than five channels done thinly.

The plan covers the whole customer journey. A buyer might find you on Google, check your reviews, visit your website, then call or fill in a form days later. So the work covers the marketing funnel end to end: being found, being trusted, and making it easy to enquire. That means your Google presence, your website, your reviews and your follow-up all working together, not as separate jobs.

We also agree the numbers the plan will be judged on, before any work starts. Enquiries, sales, revenue and cost per acquisition (CAC) matter; traffic alone does not. We also look at what a customer is worth to you over time (LTV), so we know how much an enquiry can cost and still make money, and we track return on ad spend (ROAS) wherever paid campaigns run. Together those numbers show the return on investment (ROI) of the plan, in the language a business owner actually uses: money in against money out. Where the data allows, we set up attribution in GA4, so you can see which channels bring in the customers, not just the clicks. And we keep the reporting in plain language, because a report you cannot understand is not a report.

Your brand positioning is part of the plan too. Small businesses win by being clearly different from the alternatives, not by being louder, so we agree what makes you the right choice for your buyers and carry that message through every channel, from your website to your ads.

A good example of how the channels fit together: a local home-services business might rank on Google for the services it offers, run paid ads for the jobs it wants most, collect reviews after every job, and send a short email to past customers when the season turns. No single channel does all of that. The plan is what makes them add up.

## Our Small Business Marketing process

Every engagement follows the same four steps, sized to your business.

Discover. We audit what you have: your website, your Google Business Profile, your analytics, your ad accounts, your content and your competitors. We look at who your customers are and how they find you, and we check how your brand is positioned against the alternatives they consider. The audit ends with a clear picture of where you stand, including the quick wins that can be fixed first, such as a missing service page or a Google profile that is out of date.

Plan. We build the strategy: the channels, the budget split, the timeline and the targets. The plan sets out the marketing mix for each stage of the funnel, the buyer personas for each channel, and the KPIs that will tell us whether it is working. You see the reasoning behind every recommendation, including the channels we suggest you skip for now and why. The plan is written for your budget, not for an ideal budget.

Build. We set up what the plan needs: tracking and attribution in GA4, landing pages, campaign structures, content calendars and reporting dashboards. If the plan includes local search, we fix the foundations first: your website, your Google profile and your reviews, because paid ads cannot fix a website that does not convert.

Grow. We review performance every month against the agreed KPIs, and adjust the plan as the data comes in. Channels that bring in enquiries at a good cost get more budget; channels that do not are fixed or paused. The plan is a living document, and it changes as your business grows: a new service, a new area, a new season.

## Deliverables & reporting

You always know what you are getting. An engagement delivers a written plan, the audit findings behind it, a channel-by-channel schedule with budgets and timelines, and a reporting dashboard built around your KPIs. Campaigns, content and website changes are delivered to the agreed schedule, and every deliverable is checked against the brief before it goes live.

Reporting is monthly, in plain language. Each report shows leads, sales, revenue and cost per acquisition, with the contribution of each channel. We include what changed, what we did about it and what happens next. Your consultant walks you through the report, so the numbers are always explained by a person who knows your account, and you can ask questions as you go.

We also report on the customer journey, not just the last click. Attribution in GA4 shows how search, paid media, social and content work together, so a channel that starts the journey gets the credit it deserves. And we keep your first-party data at the centre of the reporting, from your website and your email list to your customer records, so the picture improves as your data grows.

## Results / case study

Small business marketing is judged by enquiries, not by traffic. One example from our [case studies](/case-studies/) is an SEO engagement for a local service business, where a full-funnel strategy grew organic traffic by 215% in six months and multiplied leads 3.2 times. The work started with the same foundations described above: technical fixes, content built around the searches buyers actually make, and reporting tied to enquiries rather than rankings alone.

Every business is different, and we do not promise the same numbers to every client. What we do promise is that the plan will be built on your data, the targets will be agreed with you in advance, and the reporting will show whether the plan is bringing in the enquiries you need. If it is not, you will hear that from us first, with a plan to fix it.

## Pricing & engagement models

The cost of small business marketing depends on the channels in the plan and the market you are in. A focused search and paid programme for one town costs less than an omnichannel programme across several cities. We scope every engagement to your brief before work starts, so you know the price and the deliverables in advance, and there are no surprises in the first invoice.

We work in three ways. A monthly retainer covers the ongoing running of your channels, with a dedicated team and monthly reporting. A project engagement covers a defined piece of work, such as a website rebuild or a campaign launch, with a fixed scope and a fixed end date. And a consulting engagement gives you the plan and the setup, with your team doing the delivery.

Most small businesses start with a retainer on one or two core channels, then add more as the enquiries grow. That keeps the cost tied to the work, and the work tied to the results. Whichever model you choose, the proposal sets out the scope, the deliverables and the fee before you commit, and the fee is tied to the work, not to a percentage of your ad spend.
