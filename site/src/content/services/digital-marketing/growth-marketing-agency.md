## Growth Marketing Agency: what you get

Growth marketing is marketing run as a series of tests, not as a fixed plan. Every channel, every audience and every message is tried, measured and either scaled or stopped. What you get from a growth marketing agency is that loop, run every month: find what works, do more of it, and stop paying for what does not.

The work starts with your goal and your numbers. More leads, more sales, lower cost per acquisition, higher lifetime value: the goal decides the tests. From there we map the customer journey and the marketing funnel, and look for the points where the most value is lost. Often the biggest win is not a new channel but a fix in the middle of the funnel: a landing page that converts better, a follow-up email that arrives sooner, a form that asks for less.

Growth marketing uses the full marketing mix, because the answer is rarely one channel. Search and AI search bring in buyers who are already looking. Paid media tests new audiences fast. Content and social build the case over time. Email turns one-time buyers into repeat customers. And your website turns the attention into enquiries. The mix is decided by the data, not by habit, and it changes as the tests come in, so the customer journey stays omnichannel: one brand positioning, one message, wherever a buyer meets you.

Every test is judged on the same numbers: cost per acquisition (CAC), return on ad spend (ROAS), lifetime value (LTV) and return on investment (ROI). We agree the KPIs for each stage of the funnel before the work starts, and we set up attribution in GA4 so you can see which channels and which tests bring in the customers, not just the clicks. First-party data from your website and your CRM sits at the centre of the testing, so each month's results make the next month's tests sharper.

## Performance Marketing Agency

Performance marketing is the paid half of growth marketing: the channels where you pay for each click, each lead or each sale, and where the result is measured in the same week the money is spent. Search ads, paid social, programmatic and shopping campaigns all sit here, and the discipline is the same: test, measure, move the budget to what works.

A performance marketing agency earns its fee on the testing, not on the spending. We run structured tests on audiences, messages, landing pages and bids, and we report on the results in the language that matters: cost per lead, cost per sale and revenue, not impressions or clicks. When a test wins, we scale it. When it loses, we stop it and say so.

The difference between performance marketing and simply buying ads is the loop around the spend. Ads without testing drift: budgets stay where they were set, and nobody asks whether they should move. Ads with a testing loop improve every month, because the budget follows the evidence. That loop is what a growth programme adds to paid media, and it is why the two are usually bought together. You can read more about the approach in our [growth marketing](/resources/guides/growth-marketing/) guide.

## Performance Marketing Companies

There are many performance marketing companies, and they differ in what they optimise for. Some optimise for clicks, which is easy to report and means very little. Some optimise for leads, which is better but still not the whole story. The ones worth hiring optimise for revenue: qualified leads, sales and cost per acquisition, with the reporting to prove it.

When you compare companies, ask three questions. First, what do they test, and how often? A company that tests every month will beat one that set the campaigns up a year ago and left them alone. Second, how do they report? Look for reports that connect spend to sales, not just to clicks, and for attribution that shows how the channels work together. Third, who owns the numbers? A company that reports on revenue and answers for it is a partner; a company that reports on impressions is a supplier.

Dazzlit works as a performance partner, not a media buyer. We run the campaigns, we run the tests, and we report on the revenue. If a channel is not producing, we say so and move the budget, because our fee is tied to the work, not to the size of the spend.

## Our Growth Marketing Agency process

Every engagement follows the same four steps, sized to your brief.

Discover. We audit what you have: your website, your analytics, your ad accounts, your content and your competitors. We look at your customer data to find your best buyers: who they are, where they come from, what they are worth over time. We map the customer journey and find the leaks, from the first search to the repeat purchase. The audit ends with a clear picture of where growth is most likely to come from, and what to test first.

Plan. We build the strategy: the channels, the budget split, the testing plan and the targets. The plan sets out the marketing mix for each stage of the funnel, the buyer personas for each channel, and the KPIs that will tell us whether a test has won or lost. You see the reasoning behind every test, including the ones we suggest you skip and why.

Build. We set up what the plan needs: tracking and attribution in GA4, landing pages, campaign structures, content calendars and reporting dashboards. The tracking has to be right before the testing starts, because a test you cannot measure is a test you cannot learn from.

Grow. We run the tests, review the results every month against the agreed KPIs, and move the budget to what works. Winners are scaled, losers are stopped, and the learning from each test shapes the next one. The plan is a living document, and it changes as the market changes and as your data grows.

## Deliverables & reporting

You always know what you are getting. A growth engagement delivers a written strategy, the audit findings behind it, a channel-by-channel plan with budgets and timelines, a testing roadmap, and a reporting dashboard built around your KPIs. Campaigns, content and landing pages are delivered to the agreed schedule, and every deliverable is checked against the brief before it goes live.

Reporting is monthly, in plain language. Each report shows leads, sales, revenue and cost per acquisition, with the contribution of each channel and each test. We include what we tested, what won, what lost, what we did about it and what happens next. Your consultant walks you through the report, so the numbers are always explained by a person who knows your account, and you can ask questions as you go.

We report on the customer journey, not just the last click. And we keep your first-party data at the centre of the reporting, so the picture improves as your data grows and you rely less on third-party tracking.

## Pricing & engagement models

The cost of a growth programme depends on the channels in the plan, the markets it covers and how much testing it needs. A programme running several channels with a structured testing roadmap costs more than a single-channel programme, but the budget is moved every month towards whatever is producing results. We scope every engagement to your brief before work starts, so you know the price and the deliverables in advance.

We work in three ways. A monthly retainer covers the ongoing running of your channels and the testing loop, with a dedicated team and monthly reporting. A project engagement covers a defined piece of work, such as a website rebuild, a market-entry plan or a campaign launch, with a fixed scope and a fixed end date. And a consulting engagement gives you the strategy and the testing roadmap, with your team doing the delivery.

Most growth clients start with a retainer on two or three core channels, then add more as the tests identify new opportunities. That keeps the cost tied to the work, and the work tied to the results. Whichever model you choose, the proposal sets out the scope, the deliverables and the fee before you commit.
