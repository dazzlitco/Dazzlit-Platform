## Technical SEO Services: what you get

Technical SEO is the work that makes sure search engines can find, understand, and trust your website. Content and links get most of the attention, but they only work if the technical foundations are right. If Google cannot crawl your pages, cannot tell what they are about, or finds them too slow to use, even the best content will not rank. Our technical SEO service finds those problems and fixes them.

A technical SEO programme from Dazzlit starts with a full site audit — a detailed review of how your website works from a search engine's point of view. We check crawling and indexing, site structure, speed, mobile usability, duplicate content, redirects, internal linking, and schema markup. Then we fix what we find, working with your developers where needed, and we monitor the site every month so new problems are caught early.

This service suits any business whose website is not performing in search and no one can say why. It also suits businesses planning a redesign, a migration, or a move to a new platform, where technical mistakes are expensive to fix afterwards. If your content and links are already strong but your rankings are stuck, the answer is usually technical. Our [technical SEO guide](/resources/guides/technical-seo/) explains the foundations in more detail.

## Site Audit

A site audit is a full health check of your website. We crawl your site the way Google does, page by page, and record every issue that could hurt your rankings or your visitors. The audit covers the areas that matter most: whether pages are crawled and indexed properly, whether the site structure makes sense, whether pages load quickly, whether they work well on mobile, whether content is duplicated, whether redirects are correct, and whether schema markup is in place.

You receive the audit as a written report, with every issue explained in plain language and ranked by impact — what is hurting you now, what will hurt you soon, and what is worth fixing when you have time. Each issue comes with a clear fix, not just a description of the problem. We also check your site in Google Search Console, the tool that shows how Google sees your website, and we fix the errors it reports: pages that are blocked, pages that return errors, and pages that Google cannot index.

A site audit is also the starting point for any SEO programme. Without it, you are guessing at what is wrong. With it, you have a plan: a list of fixes in order of importance, and a baseline to measure progress against. If you want to check the basics yourself first, our [technical SEO checklist](/resources/checklists/technical-seo-checklist/) covers the main points.

## Technical SEO Consulting

Technical SEO consulting is expert advice for businesses that already have a team — in-house marketers, developers, or another agency — and need a specialist to guide them. Instead of doing the work for you, we tell you what to do, in what order, and why, and we review the work as it is done.

Consulting usually starts with an audit, then a plan: the fixes ranked by impact, with clear instructions your developers can follow. We stay involved while the fixes are made, answering questions, checking the work, and flagging anything that could cause problems. Common consulting jobs include site migrations, platform changes, redesigns, international rollouts, and recovering from a drop in rankings after an algorithm update.

This model works well when the problem is knowledge, not capacity: your team can do the work, but they need someone who has done it before to steer them. It also works well for one-off decisions, such as whether to change your site structure or move to a new platform. If you would rather hand the whole job to us, our [SEO services](/services/seo/) cover technical work as part of a full programme.

## Our Technical SEO Services process

Every engagement follows the same process, so you always know what is happening and why.

1. **Audit.** We crawl your site and review it against a full technical checklist: crawling, indexing, structure, speed, mobile, duplicates, redirects, internal linking, and schema markup. We also review Google Search Console and your analytics.
2. **Prioritise.** We rank every issue by impact and effort, so the fixes that matter most come first. You see the full list and the reasoning behind the order.
3. **Fix.** We fix what we can directly, and we give your developers clear instructions for the rest — with the exact changes needed, not vague recommendations.
4. **Verify.** After each fix, we re-crawl the affected pages and check Google Search Console to confirm the problem is gone and the page is indexed properly.
5. **Monitor.** We watch your site every month: crawl errors, index coverage, Core Web Vitals, and new issues as the site changes. Problems are caught early, before they cost you rankings.
6. **Report.** Every month we report on what was found, what was fixed, and what changed as a result — in rankings, traffic, and leads.

For a migration or redesign, we add a pre-launch check and a post-launch review, because that is when the expensive mistakes happen: lost pages, broken redirects, and content that disappears without anyone noticing.

## Results / case study

Technical SEO does not produce results by itself — it removes the barriers that stop content and links from working. A site with crawl errors, duplicate pages, and slow loading will underperform no matter how good its content is. Fix the technical problems, and the rest of the work starts to pay off. That is why an audit is the first step of any serious SEO programme, and why technical monitoring never stops: sites change, and new problems appear.

In one SEO engagement, we helped a local service business improve its Google rankings, attract qualified visitors, and generate more organic leads. Organic traffic rose by 215% in six months, and leads grew 3.2 times over the same period. Technical fixes were part of that work: the site had to be crawlable and fast before the content and links could do their job. You can read the full story on our [case studies](/case-studies/) page.
