## Small Business SEO Services: what you get

Small business SEO is the work of making your business easy to find when nearby customers search for what you do. When someone searches for a plumber in their town, a dentist taking new patients, or a local shop that stocks a product, the businesses that show up first get the call. Our job is to put you in that group, and keep you there.

A small business SEO programme from Dazzlit covers the whole job, not just one part of it. We start with a technical audit of your website, then fix the problems that stop Google from understanding or trusting it. We research the keywords your customers actually use, including the local searches with a town or neighbourhood in them, and build or improve the pages that should rank for them. We set up and optimise your Google Business Profile, so you can appear in the local map results as well as the ordinary listings. And we build your site's authority over time with content and links from relevant, trustworthy sources.

Everything is sized to a small business. You do not need the budget of a national brand to rank well in your own market. What you need is a focused plan: the right pages, the right local signals, and steady work month after month. That is what affordable SEO means to us — not cut corners, but a scope that matches your market and your goals, with no work you do not need. If you want to see how that scope is priced, read our [SEO pricing](/services/seo/seo-pricing-and-packages/) page, and our [SEO guide](/resources/guides/seo/) explains the basics in plain language.

## Search Engine Optimization Services for Small Business

Search engines work in three steps: crawling, indexing, and ranking. Google's crawlers visit your pages, decide what each page is about, and store it in an index. When someone searches, Google picks the pages it believes best match the search intent — what the person is actually trying to do, whether that is to buy, to find a local business, or to learn something. Our work covers every step of that chain.

First, we make sure your site can be crawled and indexed properly. That means fixing broken links, duplicate pages, slow loading, and pages that are accidentally blocked. We check your site in Google Search Console, the tool that shows how Google sees your website, and we fix the errors it reports. We also check your Core Web Vitals, the measurements of how fast your pages load and how stable they feel to use, because a slow or jumpy page loses visitors and rankings.

Second, we work on relevance. We map keywords to pages so that each important page targets the searches it can realistically win, based on keyword difficulty and how much authority your site already has. We write and improve page content around those keywords, building topical authority — a site that covers a subject properly, across many connected pages, is trusted more than a site with one thin page on it. We add schema markup, the structured data that helps Google understand your business, your services, your reviews, and your location, so you become eligible for richer results such as review stars and other SERP features.

Third, we work on trust. Google looks for E-E-A-T: experience, expertise, authoritativeness, and trustworthiness. That means real information about your business, clear authorship, honest service pages, and backlinks from other reputable sites. We also keep up with algorithm updates and the rise of AI Overviews, the AI-generated answers that now appear above the ordinary results, so your pages are written in a way that both people and AI systems can use. Good SEO for a small business is not a trick. It is a well-built website, clear content, and a steady record of being useful.

## Our Small Business SEO Services process

Every engagement follows the same process, so you always know what is happening and why.

1. **Audit.** We review your website, your Google Business Profile, your rankings, and your competitors. We look at technical health, content gaps, local visibility, and backlinks, and we agree on the targets that matter — calls, form fills, and sales, not just traffic.
2. **Keyword research.** We build a keyword map: the searches your customers use, grouped by service and location, with a realistic view of which ones you can win and in what order.
3. **Technical fixes.** We fix the issues the audit found — crawl and indexing problems, speed, mobile usability, duplicate content, and broken internal linking — so the rest of the work has a solid base.
4. **On-page and content.** We optimise your service and location pages, then publish new content where there are gaps: service pages, guides, and answers to the questions your customers ask.
5. **Local SEO.** We optimise your Google Business Profile, keep your business details consistent across the web, and build a routine for earning reviews.
6. **Authority.** We earn links and mentions from relevant local and industry sources, steadily, rather than buying links in bulk.
7. **Report and refine.** Every month we report on rankings, traffic, and leads, and we adjust the plan based on what the data shows.

The first month is mostly audit and fixes. Content and link work builds from the second month on, and the plan is reviewed with you each month so you can see progress and set the next priorities.

## Pricing & engagement models

There is no single price for SEO, because no two small businesses need the same work. What you pay depends on the size of your site, how many locations you serve, how competitive your market is, and how much content and authority building is needed. A single-location business in a small town needs a smaller programme than a business competing in several cities.

Most of our small business clients work on a monthly retainer: a fixed scope of work each month — technical monitoring, content, local optimisation, and link building — reviewed and adjusted monthly. One-off projects, such as a full audit or a website migration, are quoted separately. Every proposal lists the scope, the deliverables, and the monthly work before you commit, so you can compare it with any other quote on the basis of what is actually included.
