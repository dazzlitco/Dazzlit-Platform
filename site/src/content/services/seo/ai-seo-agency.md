## AI SEO Agency: what you get

Search is changing. People still search on Google, but more and more of them ask ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity instead — and Google itself now answers many searches with an AI Overview at the top of the page. Those answers do not show ten links. They give one answer and name a few brands. Our AI SEO service makes sure your brand can be one of the names that gets mentioned, quoted, and recommended.

An AI SEO programme from Dazzlit runs on top of your SEO foundations, not instead of them. A business that is invisible to Google is usually invisible to AI assistants too, because the assistants learn from the open web. So we start with the same work as ordinary SEO — technical health, content, and authority — and then add the work that is specific to AI search: making your pages easy for AI systems to understand, structuring your content so it can be quoted, and tracking where your brand appears in AI answers. We call this generative engine optimisation, or GEO, and it is now part of how every serious SEO programme should be built.

This service suits any business whose buyers already research with AI tools — which, by now, is most of them. If your competitors are being named in AI answers and you are not, you are losing buyers before they ever reach your website. Our [AI SEO guide](/resources/guides/ai-seo/) explains the foundations in more detail.

## Answer Engine Optimization

Answer engine optimisation, or AEO, is the work of making your business the answer when someone asks a question. It started with voice search and featured snippets — the short answers Google shows at the top of the results — and it now covers the AI answers that sit above the ordinary listings and the answers given by assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity.

The work is about clarity. AI systems quote content that answers a specific question clearly and completely, in a structure they can extract. That means pages built around real buyer questions, with direct answers up front, followed by the detail that supports them. It means structured FAQs, comparison pages, how-to guides, and definition pages, written in plain language and marked up with schema markup so the systems can understand what each page is.

It also means being right. AI systems favour content that is accurate, current, and consistent with what other trustworthy sources say. They check your facts against the web, so a page that contradicts your own site, or that is out of date, will not be quoted. Good answer engine optimisation is therefore good content work: clear answers, honest information, and pages that are kept up to date. Our [ChatGPT SEO guide](/resources/guides/chatgpt-seo/) covers how the most widely used assistant finds and quotes brands.

## AI Search Engine Optimization

AI search engine optimisation is the broader job: making your brand visible across the whole AI search landscape, not just in one assistant. That landscape includes ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, and the voice assistants that answer spoken questions. Each of these works differently, but they share one habit: they extract, synthesise, and cite, rather than ranking pages the way Google does.

Our work covers four things. First, technical readiness: making your site easy for AI crawlers to access and understand, with clean structure, fast loading, and schema markup that describes your business, your services, and your content. Second, content: pages that answer your buyers' questions well enough to quote, with the facts, comparisons, and proof that assistants look for. Third, authority: links and mentions from the sources assistants trust, because citation authority — who links to you and who quotes you — is one of the strongest signals for being named. Fourth, measurement: we run your buyers' questions through the main assistants on a schedule and record whether your brand is named and which sources the answers quote.

We run your Google work and your AI work as one plan, because they are one job: getting your brand found, quoted, and recommended wherever your buyers search. ## Generative Engine Optimization Services

Generative engine optimisation, or GEO, is the practice of making your business easy for AI systems to understand, trust, and recommend. It is the same goal as SEO — being found — but the mechanism is different. Traditional SEO earns a position in a list of results. GEO earns a mention inside a generated answer.

Our GEO services cover the full job. We start by finding out where you stand today: we ask the main assistants the questions your buyers ask and record whether your brand is named, what is said about you, and which sources are quoted. Then we fix the foundations: technical readiness, clear entity information so the systems know which business you are, and structured content that answers buyer questions directly. Then we build authority: content that earns citations, mentions in the sources assistants trust, and a consistent presence across the web so that what the assistants say about you is accurate.

We also prepare for what comes next. AI agents are starting to book and buy for people, not just answer questions, so a brand that is easy for AI systems to understand will be easier for those agents to choose. That preparation is part of the service, not an extra. ## Our AI SEO Agency process

Every engagement follows the same process, so you always know what is happening and why.

1. **Audit.** We review your website, your rankings, and your current visibility in AI answers. We ask the main assistants your buyers' questions and record where your brand is named, what is said, and which sources are quoted. We also review technical health, content gaps, and authority.
2. **Keyword and question research.** We build a list of the questions your buyers ask — in search, in AI assistants, and in sales conversations — and map them to pages, with keyword difficulty and search intent assessed for each.
3. **Technical fixes.** We fix the issues the audit found — crawl and indexing problems, speed, mobile usability, and schema markup — so both Google and AI systems can understand your site.
4. **Content.** We build or improve the pages the research calls for: service pages, FAQs, comparisons, how-to guides, and definition pages, written to be quoted as well as read.
5. **Authority.** We earn links and mentions from the sources AI systems trust, and we make sure your best content is cited where your buyers look.
6. **Report and refine.** Every month we report on rankings, AI mentions, traffic, and leads, and we adjust the plan based on what the data shows.

The first month is mostly audit and fixes. Content and authority work builds from the second month on, and the plan is reviewed with you each month.
