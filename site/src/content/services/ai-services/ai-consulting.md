## AI Consulting: what you get

AI is useful when it does a specific job better than the way you do it now — answering customers faster, writing first drafts in minutes instead of hours, or finding the pattern in data that a person would miss. It is not useful as a slogan. Our AI consulting starts from the job, not the technology, and builds only what earns its keep.

Here is what the service covers.

- **Discovery and use cases.** We look at how your marketing and sales work today and find the places where AI can help: the repetitive writing, the slow responses, the data nobody reads, the personalisation nobody has time for. Each use case is judged on the same test — what it saves, what it earns, and what it risks.
- **Generative AI and LLMs.** We work with large language models (LLMs) for writing, summarising, classifying and answering, and we set up the prompts and the checks that keep the output accurate and on-brand. AI content is always reviewed by a person before it reaches a customer.
- **AI agents and chatbots.** We build agents that handle the routine conversations — qualifying leads, answering common questions, booking calls — and hand over to a person when the conversation needs one. Our guide to [AI agents](/resources/guides/ai-agents/) explains what they can and cannot do.
- **Workflow automation.** We connect AI to the tools you already use, so the work flows without retyping: a form submission becomes a drafted reply, a new lead becomes a brief, a support ticket becomes a summary. Automation is only worth building when it removes work rather than moving it.
- **AI search visibility.** We make your brand easy for ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity to find, understand and recommend, so you appear in AI answers as well as in Google results. This is becoming as important as traditional search, and it is built into our SEO work.
- **AI governance and data privacy.** We set the rules for how AI is used in your business: what data it can see, what it may say, who checks its work, and how mistakes are caught. Governance is not paperwork for its own sake — it is what keeps a useful tool from becoming a liability.

## AI Development Company

Consulting tells you what to build; development builds it. As an AI development company, we design, build and support the software itself — the integrations, the workflows and the interfaces your team actually uses.

Development work starts with a small, working version of the idea: a pilot that proves the value before the budget grows. A chatbot that answers the ten most common questions, a content workflow that drafts and routes for review, an agent that qualifies leads overnight. If the pilot works, we extend it. If it does not, we say so and stop — a failed pilot that costs little is better than a large build nobody uses.

The systems we build connect through APIs to the tools you already run — your CRM, your email platform, your store, your analytics — so the AI works inside your business rather than beside it. We document how each system works, who owns it and what to do when it misbehaves, and we support it after launch rather than handing over a black box.

## AI Integration Services

Most businesses do not need a new platform; they need the platforms they have to work together. Our AI integration services connect AI into your existing stack, so the data flows, the tools talk to each other, and the team does not have to learn a new system to get the benefit.

Integration work usually covers three things. First, connecting the AI to your data — your CRM, your product catalogue, your content, your support history — so its answers are based on your business rather than on general knowledge. Second, connecting it to your channels — your website, your email, your chat, your social accounts — so it can act where your customers are. Third, connecting it to your reporting, so you can see what the AI did, what it cost and whether it worked.

We are careful about what we connect and what we do not. Customer data stays inside the systems you control, access is limited to what each integration needs, and nothing is sent to a third party without your agreement. Data privacy is a design decision, not an afterthought, and we make it visible in every integration we build.

## Our AI Consulting process

AI projects fail in predictable ways: a tool chosen before the problem, a pilot that never becomes a product, a system nobody trusts enough to use. Our process is built to avoid them.

**1. Discover.** We map how the work happens today — who does what, where the time goes, where the mistakes happen — and we find the use cases worth building. Each one gets a plain statement of what it should do and how success will be measured.

**2. Prove.** We build a small pilot for the strongest use case and run it with real data and real users. The pilot answers the only question that matters at this stage: does it work well enough to be worth scaling?

**3. Build.** The pilot becomes a product: integrated with your tools, governed by clear rules, and documented so your team can run it. Prompts are versioned, outputs are checked, and the handover to a person is designed in from the start.

**4. Launch and train.** We launch with the team that will use it, train them on what the system does and where it can go wrong, and stay close while the first real work flows through it.

**5. Improve.** Each month we review the numbers — time saved, quality of output, errors, usage — and we fix the biggest problem first. AI systems improve with use, but only if someone is watching and steering.

## Deliverables & reporting

Deliverables depend on the engagement, but a typical project includes the discovery findings and the prioritised use cases, the pilot, the built system with its integrations and documentation, the governance rules, and the training for your team.

Reporting matches the purpose. Each month you receive a written report covering the numbers the AI was built to move: time saved, cost per lead or per enquiry, conversion rate, and the quality of the output. The report also covers how the system is being used, where it is making mistakes, and what we are changing next. If the AI is producing content, we report on how much was used, how much was changed by reviewers, and whether it performed as well as human-written work. If it is answering customers, we report on how many conversations it resolved, how many it handed over, and how customers rated the experience.

## Pricing & engagement models

AI consulting is priced by the scope of the work: how many use cases, how many systems to integrate, and how much change the team has to absorb. A single pilot costs less than a programme that connects several systems and changes how a team works.

We work in two main ways. Most clients start with a fixed-scope discovery and pilot, which produces the use cases, the working pilot and a recommendation on what to build next. Larger programmes run on a monthly retainer, which covers the ongoing build, the integrations, the monitoring and the reporting. Either way, the proposal states the scope and the fee before work starts, and the fee reflects the actual build rather than a flat rate.
