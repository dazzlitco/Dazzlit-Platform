## Digital PR Services that drive measurable growth

Digital PR is earned media for the search era. Instead of paying for placement, we earn coverage in the publications your customers read and trust: national newspapers, trade press, broadcast, and the online outlets that journalists and buyers turn to first. That coverage builds your reputation, and it also builds your search visibility, because every quality mention can bring an authoritative backlink and a wave of referral traffic with it.

As a digital PR agency, Dazzlit plans campaigns around what journalists actually want: new data, a clear story, and a credible voice. We run data-led campaigns built on original research and surveys, react to breaking news with fast expert comment, and place thought leadership from your team in front of the right editors. The result is coverage that people read and search engines notice, measured in brand mentions, links, and a growing share of voice in your market.

Digital PR works best as part of a connected plan. Coverage earns links that strengthen your SEO, gives your [content marketing agency](/services/content-marketing/) work a wider audience, and creates the kind of third-party proof that makes paid campaigns convert better. Our [link building services](/services/link-building/) and PR teams share one plan, so the stories we pitch and the links we earn both point at the same commercial goals: more traffic, more leads, and more revenue.

A large part of digital PR is reactive. When a story breaks in your industry, journalists need expert comment within hours, and the brands that respond first get the coverage. We monitor the news and HARO-style journalist requests every day, so when a reporter asks for a source on your topic, we can put your team forward the same day. This kind of newsjacking earns links and mentions from publications that rarely respond to cold pitches, and it builds your reputation as the voice journalists call when a story develops.

We work across the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, the UAE, and Singapore, so a story can run in one market or in all six. Every campaign starts with a clear goal — a launch, a reputation to build, a search position to defend — and ends with a report that shows what the coverage delivered.

## Our digital PR process (audit → strategy → execution → reporting)

Every program follows the same four stages, so you always know where your campaign stands.

**1. Audit.** We start by reviewing your current coverage, your backlink profile, your share of voice against competitors, and what journalists have already written about you. We also look at your existing content and data to find the stories you already have but have not told yet.

**2. Strategy.** Next we agree on the goal — a launch, a reputation to build, a market to enter — and the audiences and publications that matter for it. We plan the campaigns for the quarter: data-led studies, reactive news work, thought leadership, and any launches on the calendar. You approve the plan before anything is pitched.

**3. Execution.** Our team builds the media lists, writes the pitches and releases, and contacts journalists directly. We react to breaking news the same day when a story fits your expertise, and we keep pitching until the campaign lands. Creative PR ideas are tested against one question: will a journalist want to run this?

**4. Reporting.** At the end of each month you get a report covering the coverage earned, the links gained, brand mentions, reach, and share of voice, plus the traffic and leads the coverage sent to your site. We review what worked, retire what did not, and set the next month's campaigns.

## Digital PR pricing & packages

Digital PR pricing depends on scope. The main factors are how many markets you want coverage in, how many campaigns you run each month, whether you need reactive news work and crisis support, and how much original research or data work each campaign needs. A single-market program with one campaign a month costs less than a multi-market program with data studies, thought leadership, and same-day news response.

We work on monthly retainers and on project packages for launches and one-off campaigns. There is no long-term contract: packages run month to month, and you can scale the work up or down as your plans change. Your proposal sets out the exact scope, the campaigns included, and the fee, before any work starts.
