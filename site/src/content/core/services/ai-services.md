## AI Marketing & Automation Services that drive measurable growth

AI has changed how customers find brands and how brands serve them. People now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity for recommendations instead of typing keywords into Google. At the same time, AI can answer customer questions, write first drafts, personalize campaigns, and automate the repetitive work that used to fill your team's day. The brands that win are the ones that use AI on both sides: to be found, and to serve better.

As an AI marketing agency, Dazzlit does both. We make your brand visible in AI answers — the new search — and we build the AI systems that work inside your business: chatbots that resolve customer questions, workflow automation that moves leads without manual effort, and AI-assisted content that your team reviews and publishes faster. Everything is built around your goals, not around the technology for its own sake.

The most important shift is AI search visibility. When someone asks an AI tool for the best option in your category, the answer comes from the brands the tool trusts and can cite. That trust is earned the same way search rankings are earned: with clear, accurate content, authoritative mentions, and a website the tools can read and understand. Our [seo services](/services/seo/) and AI teams share one plan, so the content, the coverage, and the technical setup all point at the same goal — being the brand the AI recommends. If you want to see where you stand today, our [llm visibility checker](/resources/tools/llm-visibility-checker/) shows how often AI tools mention your brand.

AI also raises questions every business should answer before it builds: what happens to your data, what happens to your customers' data, and who checks the AI's work. We build AI governance into every program — clear rules on which tools see which data, human review of AI-generated content before it is published, and prompts designed to keep the AI accurate and on-brand. Data privacy is part of the plan from the start, not an afterthought.

AI also makes personalization possible at a scale that manual work never could. A chatbot can greet every visitor with answers matched to their question. A workflow can send each lead the follow-up that fits their behavior. And generative AI can produce the first drafts of the content your team then edits and improves. The rule we work by is simple: AI does the heavy lifting, and people do the thinking.

## Our AI marketing process (audit → strategy → execution → reporting)

Every program follows the same four stages, so you always know where your AI work stands.

**1. Audit.** We start by reviewing how customers find you today — including how often your brand appears in AI answers — and how your team works: the repetitive tasks, the follow-ups that get missed, the content that takes too long to produce. We check your website, your content, and your tools to find the gaps AI can close.

**2. Strategy.** Next we agree on the goal — more visibility in AI search, faster customer service, more leads, or time saved — and choose the systems to build. We plan which tools to use, which workflows to automate first, and how AI content will be reviewed and governed. You approve the plan before we build anything.

**3. Execution.** Our team builds the chatbots, workflows, and content systems, connects them to your existing tools through APIs and integrations, and tests every prompt and every trigger before launch. We train your team on the systems, so they can use, review, and improve them with confidence.

**4. Reporting.** At the end of each month you get a report covering AI search visibility, chatbot conversations and resolutions, time saved by automated workflows, and the leads and sales the AI-assisted campaigns produced. We review what works, fix what does not, and plan the next systems to build.

## AI Marketing for your industry

AI marketing looks different in every industry because customers ask different questions. In eCommerce, AI chatbots answer product questions and recover abandoned carts, and AI tools can help build and manage a Shopify store — from product descriptions to store content. In B2B, AI research and personalization do the heavy lifting. In professional services, AI handles intake and follow-up so the team can focus on client work. In local businesses, a chatbot that answers questions and books appointments can be the difference between a new customer and a missed call.

Whatever your industry, the approach is the same: start with the customer, find the work AI can genuinely do better, and keep people in charge of the decisions that matter. Our [ai marketing checklist](/resources/checklists/ai-marketing-checklist/) is a good place to see what that looks like in practice, and our [marketing automation services](/services/marketing-automation/) team builds the workflows that carry the AI work into your CRM and sales process.
