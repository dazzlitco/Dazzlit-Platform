## Link Building Services that drive measurable growth

Backlinks are still one of the strongest signals Google uses to decide which pages deserve to rank. A link from a relevant, trusted site is a vote of confidence in your content, and when those votes accumulate from the right places, rankings, traffic and leads follow. Our link building services exist for that job: earning links that move the needle, not collecting numbers that look good in a spreadsheet.

Dazzlit is a link building company that works manually, one placement at a time. We research the sites that matter in your market, create or identify content worth linking to, and run outreach to the people who run those sites. No link farms, no paid link networks, no automated blasts — every link is earned from a real site, in a relevant context, with anchor text that reads naturally. That is the only way to build link equity that lasts through Google's link spam policies.

Link building works best as part of search engine optimisation, not apart from it. Our [SEO services](/services/seo/) plan which pages need authority, what those pages should rank for, and which referring domains would make the difference. Then the link building campaign goes after exactly those domains. If you already have an SEO programme, we can run link building as a standalone service alongside it. If you are starting from scratch, we combine the audit, the content and the outreach into one plan, so every link supports a page that is ready to convert the traffic it earns.

The clients who get the most from link building are the ones with something worth linking to: useful guides, original data, tools, or a genuinely good product. If that content does not exist yet, we build it first with our [content marketing agency](/services/content-marketing/) team, because outreach without linkable content wastes everyone's time. The result is a link profile that grows steadily — more referring domains, a natural mix of dofollow and nofollow links, and anchor text that matches how real people describe your business.

Law is a market where trust decides everything, and links from bar associations, legal directories, local news and respected legal publications carry real weight. Our [link building for lawyers](/services/link-building/link-building-for-law-firms/) service builds links to practice area pages and local pages, with anchor text that reflects the services and the cities you serve. ### Guest post service

Our [guest post service](/services/link-building/guest-post-service/) places original, expert articles on relevant sites in your industry, with a contextual link back to your site. We pitch the topic, write the article to the host site's standard, and place it where your audience will actually read it. Guest posts earn links and build your name in the market at the same time, and they are one of the most reliable ways to earn links from sites that never respond to a simple link request.

### Backlink audit

Every campaign starts with a [backlink audit](/services/link-building/backlink-audit/). We map your current referring domains, check your anchor text distribution, and flag toxic links — links from spammy or irrelevant sites that could hold your rankings back. Where we find harmful links, we advise on removal and, where needed, on a disavow file submitted to Google. The audit also shows where your competitors get their links, which is often the fastest way to find the domains worth approaching first.

### Niche edits services

[Niche edits](/services/link-building/niche-edits/) — also called link insertions — place your link into an existing article that already ranks and already has authority, rather than into a brand-new page. Because the page is already indexed and trusted, the link passes value faster than a fresh guest post. We only place niche edits where the link genuinely fits the article, on sites we have vetted for real traffic and relevance.

## Our link building process (audit → strategy → execution → reporting)

Good link building is a process, not a one-off purchase. Here is how every campaign runs.

### 1. Audit

We start by auditing your site and your link profile: current backlinks, referring domains, anchor text, toxic links, and the pages that most need authority. We also check domain authority — using our [domain authority checker](/resources/tools/domain-authority-checker/) alongside other quality signals — to judge which referring domains are worth pursuing and which are not worth your time. We also audit your competitors' link profiles to find the domains that link to them and could link to you. The audit sets the baseline everything else is measured against.

### 2. Strategy

From the audit we build a strategy: which pages to build links to, in what order; which tactics fit each page; which domains to target; and what anchor text mix keeps the profile natural. We agree the monthly link volume, the tactics and the reporting format with you before any outreach begins, so there are no surprises later.

### 3. Execution

Then we execute. That means finding linkable content or creating it, identifying the right contact at each target site, and running personalised outreach. Every placement is manual: we read the site, we pitch a reason the link belongs there, and we follow up. We also run broken link building — finding dead links on relevant pages and offering your content as the replacement — and resource page outreach, where your guides earn a place on curated lists. You can read more about the tactics in our guide to [how to build links to your website](/blog/how-to-build-links-to-your-website/).

### 4. Reporting

At the end of each month you get a full report: every link earned with its live URL, the referring domain, the anchor text and the target page, plus the movement in rankings, traffic and leads. We review what worked, what did not, and what changes next month. If a link is ever removed, we tell you and replace it.

## Link Building for your industry

Link building is not one-size-fits-all. The sites worth earning links from, and the content that earns them, change completely by industry.

SaaS and B2B technology companies need links from software publications, integration partners and the blogs their buyers trust. Law firms need links from legal directories, local press and bar associations. Healthcare and dental practices need links from health publications, local organisations and patient resources. Home services companies need links from local news, trade bodies and community sites. E-commerce brands need links from product roundups, gift guides and review sites. Manufacturers need links from trade publications, suppliers and distributors.

Whatever your industry, the approach is the same: find where your buyers and Google already pay attention, and earn a place there. Our [digital PR agency](/services/digital-pr/) team works alongside link building where a story, a piece of data or an expert comment can earn coverage — and links — from journalists, not just bloggers. If you are comparing approaches, our article on [link building vs digital PR](/blog/link-building-vs-digital-pr/) explains where each one fits.
