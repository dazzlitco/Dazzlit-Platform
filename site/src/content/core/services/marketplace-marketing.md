## Amazon & Marketplace Marketing Services that drive measurable growth

For many brands, the marketplace is where the buying happens. Shoppers start their product search on Amazon, compare options in the search results, and buy from the listing that looks most trustworthy. Amazon and marketplace marketing is the work of winning that search: listings that rank, content that convinces, advertising that puts your products in front of buyers, and reviews that close the sale.

Dazzlit is an Amazon marketing agency that manages the whole marketplace presence, not just the ads. We work in Amazon Seller Central every day — listings, keywords and indexing, Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display, A+ Content, the Buy Box, Fulfilment by Amazon (FBA), and the reviews that decide whether a shopper trusts you. And because most brands do not sell on Amazon alone, we plan across marketplaces: Walmart Marketplace, TikTok Shop and your own store, so each channel grows without cannibalising the others.

The numbers decide the plan. Advertising cost of sales (ACoS) tells you what the ads cost; total advertising cost of sales (TACoS) tells you what all of it costs against total sales, including the organic sales the ads create. We manage to TACoS, because an ad that looks expensive on ACoS can be the one that lifts the listing into the organic results. Marketplace fees, fulfilment costs and returns all sit in the same calculation, so the price you see in a proposal is tied to profit, not just to spend.

Expansion beyond Amazon follows the same logic. Walmart Marketplace suits brands with strong value and broad appeal, while eBay works for products with a collector or second-hand market, and each marketplace charges its own fees and sets its own rules for fulfilment and returns. We compare the options against your margins before recommending a second or third marketplace, and we keep pricing and stock consistent across them so one channel does not undercut another. The goal is not to be everywhere; it is to be profitable everywhere you are.

Our Amazon marketing services cover the listing, the advertising and the storefront around it. You can start with one service or bring them together under a single plan, and each one reports against the same targets: sales, conversion rate and the cost of acquiring them.

### Amazon PPC management

Advertising is how a new listing gets seen before it has reviews or rank. Our [Amazon PPC management](/services/marketplace-marketing/amazon-ppc-management/) service runs Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display campaigns, structured by product, keyword and match type so budgets flow to the searches that buy. We bid on the keywords your listings can actually win, defend your own brand terms, and test competitor and category targeting where it pays. Every week we move spend toward the campaigns that produce orders at an acceptable ACoS and away from the ones that do not. Our [Amazon PPC](/resources/guides/amazon-ppc/) guide explains how the auction and the metrics work if you want the detail.

### Amazon listing optimization services

A listing that does not rank does not sell, however good the product is. Our [Amazon listing optimization](/services/marketplace-marketing/amazon-listing-optimization/) service rebuilds titles, bullet points, descriptions and backend keywords around the search terms buyers actually use, so the listing is indexed for the right queries. Images come first in the work: clear main images, lifestyle shots, infographics that answer the questions a shopper asks before buying, and A+ Content that turns a plain product page into a brand page. We also work on the conversion details — price presentation, variations, stock and the Buy Box — because winning the Buy Box is what turns a ranked listing into an order.

### TikTok Shop agency

TikTok Shop sells through creators and short video, not through search. Our [TikTok Shop agency](/services/marketplace-marketing/tiktok-shop-agency/) service sets up the shop, connects creators and affiliates, and runs the live and video campaigns that put products in front of buyers who were not searching at all. The listings, pricing and fulfilment have to match what the rest of your business promises, so the shop is managed as part of the same plan as Amazon and your own store, not as a side project.

## Our Amazon marketing process (audit → strategy → execution → reporting)

Every engagement follows the same four stages, so you always know where the work stands and what happens next.

**Audit.** We start inside your Seller Central account: listings, keywords and indexing, advertising performance, reviews, Buy Box share, fees and fulfilment. We compare your listings with the competitors who rank above you and find where shoppers drop off — a weak main image, a missing keyword, a price that loses the Buy Box, or ads bidding on terms the listing cannot win. The audit ends with a written list of what to fix first, in order of impact.

**Strategy.** Next we agree the plan: the marketplaces, the products, the advertising budget and the targets. A brand launching on Amazon gets a plan weighted toward listings and launch advertising; a brand already selling gets one weighted toward defending rank, growing reviews and expanding into Walmart Marketplace or TikTok Shop. The strategy names the numbers we will be judged on — sales, ACoS, TACoS and conversion rate — so there is no argument later about what success looks like.

**Execution.** Then we build and launch: listings rewritten and re-imaged, A+ Content published, campaigns set up, storefronts improved, review requests put in place. Each marketplace is run by its specialist, but the plan is shared, so pricing, stock and promotions stay consistent across Amazon, Walmart, TikTok Shop and your own store. Our [eCommerce marketing agency](/services/ecommerce-marketing/) team runs the store side, and [eCommerce management](/services/ecommerce-management/) covers operations when the business needs hands-on help.

**Reporting.** Every month you get one report across all marketplaces, covering sales, ACoS, TACoS, conversion rate, Buy Box share and review growth. We explain what changed, why it changed and what we are doing next. If a campaign is not earning its keep, we say so and fix it or move the budget.

## Amazon Marketing pricing & packages

There is no single price for Amazon marketing, because no two sellers need the same work. What affects price is straightforward: how many products you sell, how many marketplaces you are on, how much listing work is needed, and how much advertising spend the plan requires. A small seller starting on one marketplace costs less to run than a brand selling across Amazon, Walmart and TikTok Shop.

We work in three engagement models. A monthly retainer covers the ongoing running of your listings and campaigns, with the team sized to the plan. A project covers a defined piece of work, such as a listing rebuild, a launch or a storefront build. A performance arrangement ties part of our fee to the results we agree in advance. Most clients start on a retainer and add projects as they grow. Media spend is separate from our fee and stays in your own advertising accounts, so you always own the data and the history. We recommend a starting budget based on the audit — enough to test the keywords and products that matter — and we scale it up only when the numbers justify it. If a campaign is not earning its keep, the budget moves, not the excuses. Your proposal will set out the marketplaces, the work and the price in writing before anything starts.
