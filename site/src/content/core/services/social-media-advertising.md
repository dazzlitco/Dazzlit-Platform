## Social Media Advertising Services that drive measurable growth

Organic social media builds an audience slowly. Paid social puts your business in front of the right people now — the people who match your customers, in the places they already spend their time. On Meta, TikTok, and LinkedIn, you can target by interests, behaviour, job role, and company, and you can reach people who have never heard of you but look exactly like the customers you already have.

Our paid social services are built around that targeting. We manage social media advertising for profit, not for reach. That means the campaigns are structured around the audiences most likely to buy, the creative is tested until we know what works, and the landing pages turn the click into an enquiry or a sale. Every week, we look at what each audience, ad, and format actually returned, and we move budget toward the ones that did.

Dazzlit is a global digital marketing agency, and paid social is one of the channels we run most. We manage campaigns across six markets — the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, the UAE, and Singapore — for businesses from local service companies to B2B brands. The approach is the same at every size: tight targeting, honest tracking, and reporting that shows what the spend brought back.

Paid social works best when it is not left to run on its own. The creative has to be refreshed, the audiences have to be tested, and the frequency has to be watched, or the same people see the same ad until they stop noticing it. That is the work we do every week, and it is the difference between an account that performs and one that quietly spends.

## Our paid social process (audit → strategy → execution → reporting)

Every paid social engagement follows the same four steps. The work inside each step changes with the account, but the order does not, because launching ads before the tracking is right means flying blind.

### 1. Audit

We start by looking at the account as it is — or, if there is no account yet, at the market. The audit covers the existing campaigns, audiences, creative, and tracking: whether the Pixel and Conversions API are recording conversions correctly, whether the audiences are built on real data, and whether the budget is going to the campaigns that convert. We also check the creative: how often the same people are seeing the same ad, and whether the frequency is high enough to cause fatigue. The audit ends with a list of what to fix, what to keep, and what the account should be able to return.

### 2. Strategy

From the audit, we build the strategy: the platforms to run on, the audiences to target, the campaign objectives for each stage of the funnel, and the creative each audience needs. We agree the targets with you first — the cost per acquisition, the return on ad spend, and the leads or sales the campaigns should produce — so everyone judges success the same way. We also set the testing plan: which audiences and creative we will test first, and how we will decide a winner.

### 3. Execution

Then we build and launch. Campaigns are structured so that budget flows to the audiences and ads that convert, not to the ones that merely look busy. We test creative in rotation, because the ad itself is usually the biggest lever in paid social: a better video or a clearer message can change the result more than any targeting tweak. We watch frequency and the attribution window, so we know when an ad needs refreshing and how long after a click a conversion should count. We also use remarketing to bring back the people who visited but did not convert, with a different message for those who are close to deciding.

### 4. Reporting

Every month you get a report on leads, sales, revenue, cost per acquisition, and return on ad spend, with the spend and the return shown for each campaign. We explain what changed, what we did, and what we will do next. If a campaign is not performing, we say so and change course, rather than letting it spend quietly in the background.

## Paid Social for your industry

Paid social works in every industry, but the platform and the audience have to match the way that industry's customers buy.

For B2B and SaaS companies, LinkedIn is usually the core platform, and the campaigns target the roles in the buying group with content that speaks to their problems. For ecommerce and retail, Meta and TikTok lead, with short-form video and product creative aimed at people who look like your best customers. For law firms and professional services, the work is about trust — helpful content and clear offers aimed at the people facing the problem you solve. For home services, it is local targeting and offers that make it easy to call or book. For healthcare and dental practices, it is educational content and treatment searches, with the rules of healthcare communication respected at every step.

The method is the same in every industry: understand who your customer is, find where they spend time, and test the message until it converts. If your industry is one we have not listed here, the audit will show us how your audience behaves on social media, and the strategy follows from that.

## Paid Social pricing & packages

Paid social pricing has two parts, and we keep them separate. The media spend is the money you pay to the platform for the impressions and clicks, and it goes straight to Meta, TikTok, or LinkedIn. The management fee is what you pay us to run the account, and it depends on the size and complexity of the work: how many platforms, campaigns, and audiences are involved, and how much creative testing is needed.

What affects the media budget is the cost per result in your market — LinkedIn audiences cost more per click than Meta audiences, and competitive audiences cost more than broad ones — and how many leads or sales you need each month. We recommend a budget based on those two numbers, and we will tell you honestly if the budget you have in mind is too small to produce a meaningful result.

We work on monthly retainers for most clients, because paid social needs continuous management: new creative to test, audiences to refresh, and budgets to rebalance. Project work fits a one-off audit or a single campaign build. Your proposal sets out the recommended media budget and the management fee separately, so you can see exactly where the money goes.
