Search is where most buying journeys begin. A customer types a question into Google, or asks ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity for a recommendation, and the brands that show up first, with a clear and useful answer, win the click. Our SEO services exist to put your business in that position: visible in the search results and AI answers your customers actually see, in every market you want to win.

Dazzlit is a global digital marketing agency, and SEO is the foundation of most of the plans we build. We work from data, not guesswork. Every engagement starts with an audit of your site, your rankings, and your competitors, and every recommendation is tied to the traffic, leads, and revenue it should produce. That matters because SEO is a long-term investment, and you should be able to see what it returns.

Our work covers the whole of search engine optimization. We fix the technical foundations that stop search engines from crawling and indexing your pages properly. We build content around the keywords and questions your customers actually search for, with the search intent behind each one understood before a word is written. And we build the authority — the quality backlinks, mentions, and citations — that helps Google trust your site enough to rank it. We also work on the newer side of search: making your brand easy for AI tools to find, understand, and recommend, so you appear in AI Overviews and chatbot answers as well as in the classic search results.

That is what a professional SEO service should do. Not chase rankings for their own sake, but turn search into a channel that brings in qualified visitors who become leads and customers.

## Our SEO process (audit → strategy → execution → reporting)

Every SEO engagement follows the same four steps. The work inside each step changes with the site, but the order does not, because skipping a step is how SEO projects go wrong.

### 1. Audit

We start by looking at the site as Google sees it. The audit covers technical health, crawling and indexing, existing rankings, backlinks, content quality, and the competitive gap: what your competitors rank for that you do not. We also check how you appear in AI search, including AI Overviews and the answers ChatGPT and Perplexity give for your key topics. The audit ends with a prioritized list of fixes and opportunities, ranked by the traffic and revenue they should unlock.

### 2. Strategy

From the audit, we build the strategy: the topics your site should own, the pages to create or rebuild, the technical fixes to schedule, and the authority work to run alongside. We agree the targets with you first — the keywords, the markets, and the leads or sales the work should produce — so everyone judges success the same way.

### 3. Execution

Then we do the work, in the order the strategy sets out. Technical fixes first, because they lift everything else. Content next, written around search intent and built to earn links and citations, not just to fill pages. Authority building runs throughout, focused on relevant, trustworthy sources rather than volume. We adjust as the data comes in: if a page is climbing, we strengthen it; if a topic is not moving, we find out why.

### 4. Reporting

Every month you get a report on leads, sales, revenue, and cost per acquisition, with rankings and traffic as supporting detail. We explain what changed, what we did, and what we will do next. If something is not working, we say so and change course, rather than waiting for the quarter to end.
