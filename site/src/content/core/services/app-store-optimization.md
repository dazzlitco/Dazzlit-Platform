## App Store Optimization (ASO) Services that drive measurable growth

Most apps are found in the store, not on the web. A user searches the App Store or Google Play, scans the results, and installs the app that looks most useful and most trusted. App store optimization (ASO) is the work of winning that search: the right keywords in the title, subtitle and keyword field, screenshots and a preview video that show the app working, ratings and reviews that build trust, and a listing that turns a view into an install.

Dazzlit is an ASO agency that manages the whole store presence, on the App Store and Google Play. We research the keywords your buyers actually search for, rebuild the metadata around them, design screenshots and preview video that sell the app in seconds, and run the testing — product page optimization, or A/B testing — that proves which version converts best. Where paid campaigns make sense, Apple Search Ads puts the app at the top of search while the organic listing catches up. And where the app serves more than one country, localization adapts the listing to each language and market instead of running one English page everywhere.

The numbers decide the plan. Keyword rankings tell you whether the app is found; conversion rate tells you whether the listing sells; install volume tells you whether both together are working. Ratings and reviews sit underneath all of it — a low rating will hold back even a well-ranked app. Each one is measured and reported, so you always know where the app stands.

Ratings and reviews deserve their own attention. A new user reads the most recent reviews before installing, and a run of one-star reviews about a bug or a crash will hold back even a well-ranked app. We answer reviews, route genuine problems to the product team, and watch for patterns — if several reviews mention the same issue, that is a product fix, not a marketing fix. Featuring helps too: an app that earns an editorial feature or a top chart position gets a wave of installs no keyword plan can buy, and a polished listing is what makes featuring possible.

The App Store and Google Play do not work the same way, and a listing written for one will underperform on the other. Our [Google Play Store optimization](/services/app-store-optimization/google-play-store-optimization/) service builds the listing for how Google Play actually ranks: keywords in the title and description, a short description that sells the app in one paragraph, screenshots and a feature graphic that show the app working, and the categories and tags that put it in front of the right users. Apple listings get the same care with their own rules — title and subtitle, the keyword field, and screenshots and preview video sized to the store. Both are tested, not guessed: product page optimization compares versions of the listing and keeps the one that converts.

## Our ASO process (audit → strategy → execution → reporting)

Every engagement follows the same four stages, so you always know where the work stands and what happens next.

**Audit.** We start by looking at your listing the way a user would: title, subtitle, keyword field, screenshots, preview video, description, ratings and reviews, and where the app ranks for the keywords that matter. We compare it with the apps that rank above you and find where users drop off — a weak first screenshot, a missing keyword, a low rating, or a description that never says what the app does. The audit ends with a written list of what to fix first, in order of impact.

**Strategy.** Next we agree the plan: the keywords, the markets and languages, the creative direction, and whether paid campaigns should support the organic work. An app launching in a new market gets a plan weighted toward localization and Apple Search Ads; an app with good rankings but poor conversion gets one weighted toward screenshots, preview video and product page optimization. The strategy names the numbers we will be judged on — keyword rankings, conversion rate and install volume — so there is no argument later about what success looks like.

**Execution.** Then we do the work: metadata rewritten, screenshots and preview video designed and tested, listings localized, campaigns launched, reviews answered. Each part is run by its specialist, but the plan is shared, so the promise in an ad matches the promise on the listing and the experience in the app. Where the app needs paid support, our [PPC agency](/services/ppc-management/) team runs the campaigns alongside.

**Reporting.** Every month you get one report across all apps and markets, covering keyword rankings, impressions, conversion rate, install volume, ratings and reviews. We explain what changed, why it changed and what we are doing next. If a keyword is not moving or a listing is not converting, we say so and fix it.

## ASO pricing & packages

There is no single price for ASO, because no two apps need the same work. What affects price is straightforward: how many apps you have, how many stores and languages you need, how much creative work the listing needs, and whether paid campaigns run alongside the organic work. A single app in one language costs less to run than several apps across many markets.

We work in three engagement models. A monthly retainer covers the ongoing listing work, testing and reporting, with the team sized to the plan. A project covers a defined piece of work, such as a listing rebuild, a launch or a localization programme. A performance arrangement ties part of our fee to the results we agree in advance. Most clients start on a retainer and add projects as they grow. Your proposal will set out the apps, the work and the price in writing before anything starts.
