## PPC Management Services that drive measurable growth

Pay-per-click advertising is the fastest way to put your business in front of people who are ready to buy. When someone searches for what you sell, your ad can be at the top of the page within days — not the months that SEO takes. The catch is that every click costs money, so the difference between PPC that makes a profit and PPC that burns a budget is in how the account is managed.

Our PPC management services are built around that difference. We manage Google Ads and Microsoft Ads for profit, not clicks. That means the account is structured around the searches that bring in customers, the ads speak to what those customers want, and the landing pages turn the click into an enquiry or a sale. Every week, we look at what each keyword, ad, and audience actually returned, and we move budget toward the ones that did.

Dazzlit is a global digital marketing agency, and PPC is one of the channels we run most. We manage campaigns across six markets — the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, the UAE, and Singapore — for businesses from local service companies to enterprise brands. The approach is the same at every size: tight targeting, honest tracking, and reporting that shows what the spend brought back.

If you are already running ads and they are not performing, we can audit the account first and show you where the money is going. If you are starting from scratch, we build the account properly from day one, so you do not pay to learn the lessons a good structure would have avoided.

## Our PPC process (audit → strategy → execution → reporting)

Every PPC engagement follows the same four steps. The work inside each step changes with the account, but the order does not, because launching ads before the tracking is right means flying blind.

### 1. Audit

We start by looking at the account as it is — or, if there is no account yet, at the market. The audit covers the existing structure, keywords, match types, negative keywords, Quality Score, ad copy, landing page experience, and conversion tracking. We check whether the tracking is actually recording leads and sales correctly, because an account judged on bad data makes bad decisions. The audit ends with a list of what to fix, what to keep, and what the account should be able to return.

### 2. Strategy

From the audit, we build the strategy: the campaigns to run, the keywords and audiences to target, the budget split between them, and the landing pages each campaign needs. We agree the targets with you first — the cost per acquisition, the return on ad spend, and the leads or sales the campaigns should produce — so everyone judges success the same way. We also set the testing plan: which ads and landing pages we will test first, and how we will decide a winner.

### 3. Execution

Then we build and launch. Campaigns are structured so that budget flows to the searches that convert, not to the ones that merely look busy. We write ads that speak to the searcher's intent, set bidding strategies that match the goal — whether that is leads, sales, or a target cost per acquisition — and use remarketing to bring back the visitors who did not convert the first time. Every week we review the search terms, pause what wastes spend, and move budget toward what works.

### 4. Reporting

Every month you get a report on leads, sales, revenue, cost per acquisition, and return on ad spend, with the spend and the return shown for each campaign. We explain what changed, what we did, and what we will do next. If a campaign is not performing, we say so and change course, rather than letting it spend quietly in the background.

The method is the same in every industry: understand how your customers search, build the account around the searches that convert, and judge everything on the return. If your industry is one we have not listed here, the audit will show us how your market searches, and the strategy follows from that.

## PPC pricing & packages

PPC pricing has two parts, and we keep them separate. The media spend is the money you pay to Google or Microsoft for the clicks, and it goes straight to the platform. The management fee is what you pay us to run the account, and it depends on the size and complexity of the work: how many campaigns, markets, and platforms are involved, and how much testing and landing page work is needed.

What affects the media budget is the cost per click in your market — legal and finance keywords cost far more per click than local service keywords — and how many leads or sales you need each month. We recommend a budget based on those two numbers, and we will tell you honestly if the budget you have in mind is too small to produce a meaningful result.

We work on monthly retainers for most clients, because PPC needs continuous management: new search terms to review, ads to test, and budgets to rebalance. Project work fits a one-off audit or a single campaign build. Your proposal sets out the recommended media budget and the management fee separately, so you can see exactly where the money goes.
