## Services Hub – the short answer

Dazzlit offers 20 digital marketing services in six groups: search and AI visibility, paid media, social and content, web and conversion, commerce, and reputation. One team plans and runs them together, so every channel works toward the same leads, sales and revenue.

## Why it matters / context

Most businesses do not need a single service. They need a customer journey that works: a buyer finds them on Google or in an AI answer, checks them on social media, reads their content, and then buys or gets in touch. That journey crosses several channels, and if each channel is run by a different supplier, the plan fragments. The marketing mix stops being a mix and becomes a set of separate jobs, each measured on its own numbers.

One team changes that. Search, paid media, social, content and web share one strategy, one set of buyer personas and one report. The KPIs are the same everywhere — leads, sales, revenue and cost per acquisition — so budget goes to the channels that earn it, not the ones that shout loudest.

## Key factors & examples

Start with the problem, not the channel. If buyers cannot find you, [SEO services](/services/seo/) and [local SEO](/services/local-seo/) come first. If you need leads this month, [PPC management](/services/ppc-management/) and [social media advertising](/services/social-media-advertising/) can deliver while search builds. If the website loses visitors who arrive, [conversion rate optimisation](/services/conversion-rate-optimization/) and [web design and development](/services/web-design-development/) fix the leak before more traffic is bought.

The six groups cover the full funnel. Search and AI visibility put you where buyers look, including ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. Paid media buys attention with a return to prove. Social and content, including [content marketing](/services/content-marketing/), [media production](/services/media-production/), [link building](/services/link-building/) and [digital PR](/services/digital-pr/), earn the attention that ads cannot buy. Web and conversion turn visits into enquiries. Commerce services — [ecommerce management](/services/ecommerce-management/), [ecommerce marketing](/services/ecommerce-marketing/) and [marketplace marketing](/services/marketplace-marketing/) — grow stores and listings. Reputation services, [reputation management](/services/reputation-management/) and [review generation](/services/review-generation/), make sure what buyers find about you helps rather than harms.

Two more groups round out the offer: [marketing automation](/services/marketing-automation/) for the follow-up that turns enquiries into customers, [app store optimisation](/services/app-store-optimization/) for apps, and [AI services](/services/ai-services/) for brands that want AI search visibility and AI-assisted marketing built into the plan. Attribution and GA4 sit underneath all of it, with first-party data used to measure what matters: ROI, ROAS, customer acquisition cost and lifetime value.

## Mistakes to avoid

The most common mistake is buying channels before agreeing what they must achieve. A business that hires for traffic gets traffic, not sales. Agree the goal first — leads, revenue, cost per lead — and let the channels serve it.

The second is judging each channel alone. Social media rarely closes a sale on its own, and search rarely creates demand on its own. Judge the funnel as a whole, and give brand positioning time to work: the channels that build trust often look weak on last-click attribution and strong everywhere else.

The third is treating services as interchangeable. Technical SEO, paid social and TikTok ads are different crafts. Ask who will do the work, and how often it is reviewed, before you sign.
