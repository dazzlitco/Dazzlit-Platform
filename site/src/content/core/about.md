## About Dazzlit – the short answer

Dazzlit is a global digital marketing agency working in six markets — the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, the UAE and Singapore — with 20 services run by one team of specialists, measured on leads, sales and revenue.

## Why it matters / context

Most brands end up juggling a specialist for each channel, and the plan falls apart between suppliers. Dazzlit was built the other way round: one agency, one plan across every channel, so search, paid media, social, content and web pull in the same direction. The idea behind the name is simple — ideas that travel, brands that grow — and the work is organised to make that true in each market, not just in one.

The agency works the way its clients' buyers behave. Customers search on Google, ask ChatGPT, scroll TikTok and shop on Amazon, often in the same day. A plan that covers only one of those places misses the rest of the customer journey. So Dazzlit plans the whole funnel, from the first time a buyer hears of a brand to the repeat purchase, and reports on the numbers that decide budgets.

## Key factors & examples

The team is built from specialists, not generalists. Daniel Carter, Founder and CEO, leads the agency's vision, client partnerships and overall business strategy. Sophia Martinez, Head of Marketing, oversees marketing strategy and coordinates campaigns. James Wilson, SEO Specialist, develops search strategies that improve organic visibility, rankings and qualified traffic. Emily Davis, Content Strategist, plans content that communicates each client's value. Arjun Patel, Paid Ads Specialist, manages paid campaigns and looks for ways to improve return on ad spend. Olivia Kim, Web Design Lead, leads website design and user experience.

Each client gets a team built around them: the right industry experience, the right channel specialists and working hours that overlap with theirs. The work follows one approach — Discover, Create, Grow — from the first audit to monthly reporting. Discover means auditing the channels, researching the audience and competitors in each market, and agreeing the targets that matter. Create means building the strategy, creative and content for each market and channel. Grow means running, testing and improving every month, and reporting against leads, sales and revenue.

That structure covers 17 industries, from healthcare and law firms to SaaS, home services and nonprofits, with playbooks built around each sector's buyers, rules and seasons. Across all of them, the measures are the same: KPIs that track the funnel, attribution and GA4 to show what each channel contributed, ROI, ROAS, customer acquisition cost and lifetime value — and brand positioning judged on whether it earns trust, not just attention. ## Mistakes to avoid

The first mistake when choosing an agency is judging it on a single channel. A team that is strong in paid media but weak in search, or strong in content but weak in measurement, will leave gaps in the funnel. Ask how the channels work together and how the whole plan is reported.

The second is choosing on price alone. The cheapest quote is often the one with the least senior people on it, the thinnest reporting and the most room for surprise fees. Ask who will do the work, how often it is reviewed, and what is included before you compare prices.

The third is ignoring fit. An agency that cannot explain your buyers, your market and your competition in plain language will not market to them well. A short conversation tells you more than a long proposal: ask what they would do first, and why.
