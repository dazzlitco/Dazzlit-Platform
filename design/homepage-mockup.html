Ask a question
What does a digital marketing agency do?

A digital marketing agency plans and runs the channels that bring you customers online: search (SEO and AI search), paid ads, social media, content, email and your website. Dazzlit runs these as one plan, so every channel works toward the same leads and sales targets.

How much does digital marketing cost?

It depends on your goals, your market and how many channels you need. Most clients start with one or two core channels and add more as results come in. Placeholder [starting price or package range from the pricing page]

Which countries do you work in?

We work with brands in the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, the UAE and Singapore, and plan launches into new markets for clients expanding abroad.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Most sites see early movement within 3 months and meaningful growth in leads within 6 to 12 months. It depends on your competition, your site's history and how much content you need.

Can you get my brand recommended by ChatGPT and other AI tools?

Yes. AI search visibility is part of our SEO work: we make your brand easy for ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity to find, understand and cite, and we track how often you appear in their answers.

Do you work with small businesses or only large brands?

Both. We work with growing small businesses, B2B companies and enterprise brands, and size the team and budget to the goal.
